README.md

15b Pre-Webinar Nurture (WhatsApp, Email, Vodex)

Every message a registrant gets from the moment they register until 10 minutes into the live session. The webinar is weekly, so everything is timed from webinar start, with rules for late registrants.

What Is Here

File What it is
SEQUENCE_MAP.md The timeline, who gets what, late-registrant and sending-hour rules, exits
COPY.md Every email and WhatsApp message in full, for reading
email.csv, whatsapp.csv, vodex.csv Import files, one per channel: step_id, trigger, offset_minutes, channel, template_name, subject, body, variables
emails/index.html The 13 HTML emails with a gallery (no confirmation email: WebinarJam sends it) (brand palette, current Tantra mark)
vodex/VODEX_CALL_SCRIPT.md Call script: opening, purpose, confirm, can't make it, calendar, voicemail; triggers and outcomes
vodex/agent-settings.json The two Vodex agent settings (greetingMessage, context, rules)
vodex/trigger-call.example.json Shape of the call request (invented person)
build.py The one source of copy. Edit it, run python3 build.py, and every CSV, email and COPY.md is rebuilt and checked
scheduler.py The timing rules in code. python3 scheduler.py --selftest runs 10 registration cases plus the WhatsApp window checks
nurture_lib.py Shared builder for 15b, 22 and 27: config tokens, WhatsApp template checks, HTML emails

How to Use It

  • Facts from funnel-config.json (host name, call length, session length, offer) are written in at build time. Change the config, rerun build.py.
  • Merge fields stay as {{name}} in the CSVs and emails: first_name, webinar_title, webinar_date_local, webinar_time_local, webinar_when_local, join_url, calendar_url, watch_url, video_*_url, booking_url, unsubscribe_url, company_address. Where each comes from: VARIABLES in nurture_lib.py. {{join_url}} is each registrant's personal WebinarJam link, merged from the contact (the register Worker, item 17, posts it on webinar.registered); the generic room link in config is only the fallback.
  • WhatsApp: bodies use positional variables ({{1}}, {{2}}) and the variables column says what each number is, so they can be submitted as WhatsApp Business templates (language English; category in the trigger column). Tantra sends from the number connected in Tantra; whether Tantra needs pre-approved templates is unverified, so the wording follows template rules either way.
  • Tantra: one campaign per timed message (email step plus WhatsApp step at wait 0), keyed by template_name; the runner adds each registrant at the time scheduler.py gives.

What Adi Must Fill (funnel-config.json)

webinar.join_url (the generic room link, a fallback only); domains.funnel_domain; media.indoctrination_videos (4); booking.round_robin_url; brand.legal_footer_address; integrations.vodex_agent_id and vodex_caller_number; the sending mailbox (not on growthclub.org, usetantra.com or free Gmail).

Notes for the Lead

  • WhatsApp window: every WhatsApp step, live-day reminders included, goes only 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone; outside it only the email goes. On the decided schedule (Thursday 10:00 UK time, 11:00 CET; first session 12 November 2026) UK registrants get 15B-H3 (3 hours before, 07:00 UK) by email only; every other live-day step keeps WhatsApp. Central Europe and India keep WhatsApp on every step; US East Coast registrants (05:00 local) get the live-day reminders by email only. Waiving the window for 15B-H3 is Adi's decision (the task board). Day-level steps go on weekdays at 10:00 UK (11:00 CET), never on a weekend: SEQUENCE_MAP.md, "On the UK Clock".
  • WhatsApp categories: Utility for reminders; Marketing for 15B-D3 and 15B-X1 (the trigger column of whatsapp.csv says which). 15B-R0 and 15B-X2 are kept to 3 and 2 variables.
  • 15B-L0 and 15B-L10 use WebinarJam's live attendance to skip people already in the room (item 17). Without it, L10 goes to everyone and its copy says "Already in? Ignore this."
  • 15B-C1 targets "opened no 15b email": needs open tracking in Tantra; if Tantra has no opens, use "clicked no link and no reply".
  • 15B-V4 mentions the Bonuses video but names no bonus: offer.webinar_bonus_stack is not approved yet.

No "Claim to verify" in this folder: no unsourced claim is printed. Proof used: Growth Idea best webinar (PROOF_PACK section 3).

Who Gets What

  • Email: every registrant, from 15B-R1 on (the confirmation email is WebinarJam's). Each email carries the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
  • Join link: {{join_url}} merges from the contact: the personal WebinarJam link the register Worker adds to webinar.registered. The generic room link (webinar.join_url) is a fallback only.

  • Template categories: Utility for every reminder; Marketing for 15B-D3 (client proof) and 15B-X1 (evaluation call link).
  • Calls: only registrants who ticked the call box (call_opt_in, see README contract note), with a valid phone, inside 08:00 to 20:00 in their own timezone. At most two calls per webinar.
  • Quick replies (YES, CAL, CANT) post contact.quick_reply (CRM_CONTRACT): they do not stop the reminders. CAL sends X2. CANT moves the contact to the replay track and sends X1. Any other reply goes to a person (posts contact.replied).

Late Registrants

  1. Registered after the start: only 15B-L0 (we're live) goes out, at once, and only while the session is still running (before start + webinar.duration_minutes). Registered after it ended: nothing from 15b; the item 22 no-show track or the next date applies.
  2. Past steps are skipped. Registered 4 days before? W1 and V2 never send.
  3. Missed the first video email? 15B-R1 sends all four videos as one page, 60 minutes after registering, if that is still 26 hours or more before T.
  4. No pile-ups. A step due less than 30 minutes after another planned message is skipped. Exceptions: 15 minutes, live now and late +10, because they carry the link when it matters. Example: registered at T minus 3h10m gets the confirmation, then not the 3-hour reminder 10 minutes later.

Sending Hours

The registrant's timezone is timezone on webinar.registered (IANA, from the registration page's browser). When it is missing, the webinar timezone (webinar.timezone) is used.

  • WhatsApp, every step, live-day reminders included: only 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone (CRM_CONTRACT). Outside the window the email still goes and the WhatsApp is skipped. Waiving this for live-day steps is Adi's decision.
  • Day-level steps (W1, V2, D3, V4, D1) move into the window, both channels: before 08:00 moves to 08:00, from 20:00 moves to 19:00, same local date.
  • Event-hour steps (H3, H1, M15, L0, L10) go at their time by email; their WhatsApp goes only if that time is inside the window.
  • Calls always keep to 08:00 to 20:00 local. C1 moves into the window or is skipped; C2 is skipped.

Exits and Changes

  • Opted out (any channel): every step stops.
  • Track moved to replay (CANT quick reply or call outcome cant_attend): D1 to L10 and both calls stop; the item 22 no-show track still sends the recording.
  • Re-registered for a later date: pending steps for the old date are cancelled and the plan is rebuilt for the new date.
  • Booked an evaluation call before the webinar: reminders continue (they should still attend).

Building It in Tantra

Same pattern as context/from-workshop-project/15b-pre-nurture/nurture.md: a Tantra step waits whole days, so each timed message is its own campaign (email step plus WhatsApp step, both at wait 0), keyed by template_name. The runner (item 23) adds each registrant to the campaign at the time scheduler.py gives, with the per-person fields in the variables column as Tantra custom fields. Campaign schedule open every day, every hour, in the webinar's timezone.
