VSL Script: Thank-You Page (item 14)

Speaker: Adi. Runtime: about 2 minutes 30 seconds (about 360 spoken words at 150 words a minute). Where it plays: the video slot in step 1 of /thank-you/ (media.vsl_video_url in funnel-config.json). Delivery: calm, direct, to camera. No music bed under the proof lines. Captions on (the player starts muted). Visual: Adi's AI avatar or Adi to camera; on-screen text only where marked.

[0:00 Hook]

You're in.

Before you close this tab, give me two minutes. What you do in the next five minutes decides whether this training changes anything for you.

[0:15 The pain]

Here's what I see over and over.

A founder has a good offer. Referrals got them here. But every month starts at zero. The pipeline depends on who they happen to meet, and they're still the one chasing every deal.

If that sounds like you, you're in the right place.

[0:35 Who I am]

I'm Adi. I founded Tantra and GrowthClub. For the last decade I've built revenue engines for other people: 400+ brands across 21+ countries and 47+ industries.

On screen: 400+ brands. 21+ countries. 47+ industries.

[0:50 What the training covers]

In the live session I'll show you the funnel my team builds: calendar invites that fill a session every week, reminders that get people there, and a pipeline that books calls without you chasing each one.

It's the funnel we ran for Growth Idea, a UK coaching group. Over 11 webinars it reached 765,105 people, registered 6,517 and booked 80 sales calls. Those are measured counts from their webinar tracker.

On screen: Growth Idea, 11 webinars, May 2025 to Feb 2026: 765,105 reached, 6,517 registered, 80 sales calls booked.

[1:20 Why come live]

Come live. The last fifteen minutes are your questions, about your business, not a made-up one. That part only happens live.

[1:30 The three steps]

Three things to do right now, all on this page.

One: add the training to your calendar. One click.

Two: join the WhatsApp community, so your join link and reminders reach you where you'll see them.

Three: press Watch the 4 videos at the bottom of this page. Funnels, the RevOps operating system, traffic channels, and what you get if we build it with you. Five to eight minutes each.

On screen: Add to calendar. Join WhatsApp. Watch the 4 videos (bottom of this page).

[2:05 What to bring]

And have one thing ready when we start: your rough numbers from last month. How many people you reached, how many booked a call, how many bought.

Don't have them? That's fine. Finding out you don't is the first lesson.

[2:25 Be straight about the offer]

I'll be straight with you. At the end I'll explain Tantra Enterprise, where my team builds this funnel with you over three months. It's for founders with $10,000 or more to invest in growth over the next year.

If that's not you, the training still gives you the whole map.

See you live.
