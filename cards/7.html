CUSTOMISE.md

How To Customise a Post for One Group

  1. Read the group's rules and pinned post. Copy the rule on links and promotion into the tracker's rules column.
  2. Take the approved idea for that group from GROUPS.md (the post itself comes from the content studio) and its "Can we post ourselves?" line.
  3. Admin approval needed for promotion? Ask the admin first, in the way the rules say.
  4. Rewrite the first line in the group's own words: its niche, or a question members asked this week.
  5. Regional groups: fill {{region}}, and give the time in the members' own zone next to UK time (for example "10:00 UK time, 11:00 in Berlin"; outside Europe convert it first: 15:30 in India).
  6. Tag the link utm_content=fb_<group-slug> so registrations show which group sent them.
  7. Never post identical text in two groups on the same day, and post in at most 3 groups a day.
  8. Reply to every comment the same day.
  9. Log the posted date, placement and post URL in group-tracker.csv; add registrations by utm_content a week later.
