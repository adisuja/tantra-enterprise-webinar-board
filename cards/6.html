MANUAL TEAM STEP, only after Adi approves the approval page (index.html). Done by a super admin of the page (only a super admin can change the name and the URL). Copy for every field is in COPY.md (generated from copy.json); images are in assets/. Do the steps in this order.

Before You Start: LinkedIn's Name-Change Rules

From LinkedIn Help "If you want to change your LinkedIn Page or Showcase Page name" (https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a566295), read 2026-11-04: - GrowthClub.org to Tantra is a "significant name change" (it is not a typo fix, a merger or an acquisition). - With a small number of associated employees, you change it yourself under Edit page. With a large number, you must contact LinkedIn and they review it. LinkedIn does not publish the cut-off; the page shows 25 associated members today. - If the page is verified, every name change goes through LinkedIn, whatever its type. - Followers stay: the page keeps its followers, posts and page id; only an acquisition, merger, de-merger or split blocks follower migration. - The URL does not change when the name changes. The URL can change at most once every 30 days. After a change both the old and new URL reach the page until another page requests the old one (https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564298). - 100-character limit on the name.

What Followers See

  • The 8,810 followers keep following; the page name, logo and cover change everywhere it appears (feed, follow lists, mentions, old posts).
  • LinkedIn Help does not say whether followers are told about a rename, so the announcement post in COPY.md tells them.
  • Members who list GrowthClub.org as their employer keep that text until they edit their own profile; LinkedIn says to post and ask them to update.

Step 1: Open the Admin View

  1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com/company/13588687/admin/dashboard/ (signed in as a super admin of GrowthClub.org).
  2. Check the role: left menu, Settings, Manage admins. Your row must say Super admin.

Step 2: Name, URL and Tagline (Page info)

  1. Left menu: click Edit page. The Page info tab opens (newer layout: the Header section).
  2. Name: replace "GrowthClub.org" with Tantra.
  3. LinkedIn public URL: leave it as growthcluborg for now if you will ask Support for usetantra (Step 6). Otherwise type tantrahq. If the box says it is taken, try tantra-hq, then tantraai.
  4. Tagline: paste the tagline from COPY.md (111 characters).
  5. Logo: click the logo box, upload assets/tantra-logo-400-light.png, keep the crop as it is, Apply.
  6. Click Save (top right).
  7. What happens next: the new name shows at once. If LinkedIn blocks the name (a "contact us" message, or the Name box is locked), go to Step 7.

Step 3: About, Website, Industry, Size, Specialties (Details)

  1. Still in Edit page, click the Details tab (newer layout: the About section).
  2. Overview (newer layout: Description): delete the old text, paste the About text from COPY.md (1,593 characters). Until the registration page is live, leave out the two lines named under it in COPY.md.
  3. Website: https://usetantra.com
  4. Industry: delete "Marketing Services", type Technology, Information and Internet, pick it from the list.
  5. Company size: keep 11-50 employees (guidance in COPY.md).
  6. Company type: keep Privately held. Year founded: keep 2018 unless you decide otherwise.
  7. Specialties: remove all 17 old ones (the x on each), then add the 14 from COPY.md one at a time.
  8. Click Save.

Step 4: Cover Image

  1. Back on the admin view (https://www.linkedin.com/company/13588687/admin/dashboard/), click the pencil on the cover image.
  2. Upload assets/tantra-cover-4200x700.png (hi-res, 0.4 MB, under LinkedIn's 3 MB limit). If LinkedIn refuses it, upload assets/tantra-cover-1512x256.png (the size LinkedIn Help a563309 states). Do not zoom or move it. Click Save (or Apply).
  3. What happens next: check the page on your phone; the text sits in the centre, clear of the logo, so it survives the mobile crop.

Step 5: Custom Button (Buttons)

  1. Edit page, Buttons tab, switch Custom button on.
  2. Before the registration page is live: choose Visit website, URL https://usetantra.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=company_page.
  3. Once domains.funnel_domain is set: choose Register, URL https://webinar.sellwithtantra.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=company_page&utm_campaign=enterprise-webinar&utm_content=button, and swap the About's last line back to the registration link version in COPY.md.
  4. Click Save.

Step 6: The Existing "Tantra" Page and the usetantra URL (decide first)

A page named Tantra already lives at https://www.linkedin.com/company/usetantra (founded 2024, 2 employees shown, the same tagline as GrowthClub). After Step 2 there will be two pages called Tantra. - If that page is yours: open LinkedIn Support at https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a564298, use Contact us at the bottom, and ask them to move the public URL usetantra from that page to page id 13588687. Then deactivate or keep the old page (its followers do not move with the URL). - If it is not yours: use tantrahq and leave it.

Step 7: Only If LinkedIn Blocks the Name Change

  1. Open https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a566295 and click Contact us at the bottom.
  2. Say: page id 13588687, current name GrowthClub.org, new name Tantra, reason: the company now trades as Tantra (https://usetantra.com); same company, same owner, not a merger or acquisition.
  3. LinkedIn reviews and replies by email with next steps. Carry on with Steps 3 to 5 meanwhile; they do not depend on the name.

Step 8: Announce

Post the rename announcement from COPY.md from the page, with the link in the first comment. Ask the team to update the employer on their own profiles.

Step 9: Pinned Post

  1. On https://www.linkedin.com/company/13588687/admin/dashboard/ click Create, then Start a post.
  2. Paste the pinned post text from COPY.md; add the image assets/tantra-pinned-post-1200x627.png. Click Post.
  3. On the published post, click the ... menu, then Pin to top (older layout: Feature on top of page). It stays first on the page until you change it.

Step 10: One LinkedIn Event per Thursday (recommended)

  1. Same admin view, Create, then Event.
  2. Cover image assets/tantra-event-cover-1776x444.png; Event type Online, format External event link; Event name, description and external link from COPY.md; date the coming Thursday, 10:00, timezone (UTC+00:00) London; 60 minutes. Click Create.
  3. Share the event from the page and ask the six senders to share it. Repeat each week (LinkedIn events are one date each).
