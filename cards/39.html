Card 39: the funnel map

One page that shows every step of the Enterprise webinar registration flow, every email and WhatsApp message wired to it, and every bonus and password. It answers when each message sends and what it says, and you can copy any of it from the page.

Live, open to anyone with the link (no password, Adi 2026-11-08): https://tantra-funnel-map.growthcluborg.workers.dev/

What is on it

  • Map. 10 steps top to bottom: registration, survey thank-you (62%), RevOps OS upsell (73%), Dodo checkout, confirmation (100%), Revenue Calculator, countdown, live session, bonus site, replay. Each page is a cover built from a real screenshot; click it to open the live page and its states. Every message is connected to the step that triggers it.
  • Side panel. Click any message: when it sends (UK time for Thursday 12 November), the trigger, who gets it, the sender, the status, then the subject, preview and body, each with a Copy button. WhatsApp reminders show the 1-to-1 text and the Announcements group text side by side.
  • Timeline. All 27 messages in send order.
  • Bonuses and passwords. The 3 free bonuses and the paid upsell, the one bonus password, where it is revealed and where it is sent.

The sequence (as of 9 November, night)

Pages open on https://webinar.sellwithtantra.com. Email is sent only by WebinarJam (7 emails from Adi Suja adi@jointantra.com: confirmation, 3 days, 1 day, 1 hour, 5 minutes, then bonuses to attendees and the replay to no-shows 2 hours after). WhatsApp 1-to-1 is sent by the Tantra CRM Automation from +1 445 622 7661 (5 Webinar sequences fed by the site Worker through crm-api.usetantra.com/intake/webinar); each template step waits for Meta approval and is skipped once past its allowed delay. Group posts run from the card 36 scheduler. No webinar SMS. Messages not sent by decision (E1, E2A, E2B, E-OS1, E3, E-L0, WA2) stay on the map marked Not sent.

Bonus password rule: revealed only live, sent after the session only to people who came (E-P1, W-P1), shown to no-shows in the replay's Bonuses chapter, never posted in the WhatsApp group.

Build

src/funnel.js holds every page, message, bonus, merge field and template name; src/index.html renders it; tools/check.mjs checks every message and exports the CSVs. bash publish.sh builds into site/ (git-ignored), fills the bonus password from ~/.config/tantra/keys/tantra-bonuses.env, stops on any em or en dash, and deploys the Worker tantra-funnel-map, which serves the site with no password. tools/shoot.py refreshes the page covers; tools/verify.py screenshots every view.
