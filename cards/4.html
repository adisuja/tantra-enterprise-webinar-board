Notes on the math:

  • Sales Navigator hits Tantra's weekly ceiling. 2x gives 40 requests a day, 200 a week, but Tantra stops every account at 180 a week. Content engagement keeps its 50, so connector gets 180 minus 50 = 130 a week (about 26 a day). 40 a day is also above Tantra's 20 to 30 guidance for Sales Navigator, so step up to it over two weeks (below).
  • Free matches Tantra's free guidance: 20 requests a day (guidance 15 to 20, without a note) and 100 a week (guidance up to 100).
  • Connector follow-ups are the messages to people who accepted. They are budgeted at one a day per request sent (15 free, 30 Sales Navigator). If more queue up, Tantra moves them to the next day.
  • Messenger sends 3 messages per person, so 20 messages a day is about 7 new people a day in steady state (13 on Sales Navigator).
  • InMail (10 a day) is our recommendation. Sales Navigator gives 50 credits a month (about 2 a sending day), so about 2 a day go on credits and the rest only to Open Profiles, which use no credit. If the list has too few Open Profiles, drop to 2 a day.
  • Connection notes. Free accounts get about 3 personalised notes a month, so every request from a free account goes without a note. Notes switch on with Sales Navigator.

Tantra Fit

  • Step actions. LinkedIn steps are visit_profile, connection_request and send_message (Tantra source, src/lib/channels/types.ts, clone of 2026-10-11). The Tantra CSVs hold only these; build.py fails on anything else. CSV columns map to the step fields: delay_days to the wait, action to the step action, message to the template. plan_rule says whether a row runs on free, on Sales Navigator only, or (for notes) goes without a note on free.
  • InMail. That source has no InMail step. csv/inmail_step1.csv holds step 1: if Tantra shows an InMail step once a Sales Navigator account is connected, load it there; if not, the sender sends it by hand in Sales Navigator and, 5 days later, imports only non-repliers into the follow-up campaign (csv/inmail_followup.csv).
  • Limits are per account. Tantra caps each LinkedIn account, not each campaign. The account limits above are the sums; the per-campaign split is kept by batch size (the team steps in README.md).
  • Engager lists. Tantra has no "people who engaged with this post" step. Each post's reactions and comments are exported to a CSV outside the repo (first name, LinkedIn URL, degree, post_topic) and imported with post_topic added as a custom field.
  • Merge tags. {{firstName}} and {{company}}, as documented on https://usetantra.com/help/templates-and-ai/merge-tags-and-spintax. An unknown or empty tag renders blank. If {{custom.post_topic}} renders blank in the test, type the phrase into that post's campaign.
  • Slots. Each connected LinkedIn account uses one social account slot on the Tantra plan. Six senders need six slots.
