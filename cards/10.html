IG2. The Outreach Domain Quest

  • Source: BR3 (sample HD22, retro handheld-game levels, pixel art, final boss "The Lapse").
  • Format: Carousel, 10 images.
  • Hook: Your sending domain takes weeks to earn a reputation and one missed renewal to lose it. Four levels to beat.
  • Lesson: Name it (a .com that reads as your brand, no hyphens, never your main domain), register it with auto-renew decided on purpose, name the inboxes after real senders, redirect it to your main site, and never let a warmed domain lapse.
  • Proof: None (method only).
  • Webinar tie: Link in bio: the sending setup is where the session's build starts.
