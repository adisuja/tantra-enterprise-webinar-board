The Member's System: How Every Piece Connects

This is what a 100K MRR Club member ends up with, and how the Funnel OS chat runs the other systems. The course videos teach it; this page is the spec the team builds against.

1. The Systems and Who Does What

System Job How Funnel OS reaches it Member sets up once
Funnel OS (chat app) The member's workspace. Holds the Funnel Brief, writes every asset, builds and deploys pages, drafts campaigns and sequences, reads numbers back, runs the weekly review n/a (it is the hub) A project, the Funnel Brief, the connections below
Tantra app (usetantra.com) Outreach: cold email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, calendar invites, omnichannel sequences, reply inbox Tantra REST API, webhooks and the Tantra MCP server; API key from Connectors > API Keys Tantra Pro plan, mailboxes through Google delegation, LinkedIn sign in, WhatsApp QR, calendar senders
Tantra CRM (crm.usetantra.com) Contacts, pipelines, booking pages, sequences (email, WhatsApp, SMS, AI call, task, Slack), tracked links, payment connections, dashboards The CRM MCP tools and API key A workspace, Slack (optional), WhatsApp sender, the tracking snippet domain
Content OS (content.usetantra.com) Content context, batches, images, approval, publishing to every channel Content OS API key Publishing accounts per channel (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, company pages)
Payments Checkout and money in the bank Provider API key (Dodo, Razorpay or a Stripe restricted key) and a signed webhook into the CRM Business verification, bank account, product and price
Cloudflare Domains, DNS, page hosting API token with Zone, DNS, Redirect, Registrar, Workers and D1 permissions Account, billing, registrant contact
Google Workspace Sending mailboxes and calendar invite senders Through Tantra (delegation) Workspace accounts on outreach domains
Analytics GA4, Clarity Service account on GA4; snippet on pages Property and project IDs

The approval rule. Funnel OS drafts and prepares everything. It never sends, launches a campaign, buys a domain, charges a card or deletes anything until the member types an approval in their own words in the chat. Every such step is logged in the Funnel Brief.

2. From First Touch to Money in the Bank

Step Event From To What changes
1 Prospect gets a touch (email, LinkedIn, calendar invite, WhatsApp, post, ad) Tantra app, Content OS, ads Prospect Link is a CRM tracked short link with UTMs
2 Click Tracked link Landing page CRM records source, campaign, A or B
3 Sign-up (form) or calendar RSVP Yes or Maybe Page form, Tantra RSVP webhook Tantra CRM Contact created, tag registered, deal at Form Filled, consents saved
4 Confirmation and reminders Tantra CRM sequence Email, WhatsApp, AI call Steps stop on booking, purchase or STOP
5 Attends, misses or watches replay Webinar platform or video page Tantra CRM Tag attended, no-show or replay-watched; branch sequences
6 Reply Tantra app reply webhook Tantra CRM Tag replied; task for a same day answer with the booking link
7 Books a call CRM booking page Tantra CRM Deal to Call Scheduled; pre-call sequence; Slack alert
8 Call held, qualified, proposal sent Closer in the CRM Tantra CRM Stage moves; proposal follow-ups at 48 h and 7 days
9 Pays Dodo, Razorpay or Stripe checkout Signed webhook to the Tantra CRM Deal to Payment Completed with value; buyer leaves every chasing sequence; welcome sequence starts
10 Money lands Provider payout Member's bank Payout schedule per provider; the CRM Revenue dashboard shows collected revenue
11 Did not buy Tantra CRM Long-term nurture Weekly letter, WhatsApp twin, next event invite, reactivation at 90 days

Rule taught in V13: tools report events and events move deals. Any deal a person had to move by hand marks a step that is not wired.
