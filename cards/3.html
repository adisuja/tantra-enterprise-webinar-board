ADI_STEPS.md

Team Manual Setup Steps: Item 3, Cold Calendar Senders

This setup is a manual job for the team. Everything an agent could do is done: the plan, 16 checked names, every DNS record, the 72-sender roster. These steps need a sign-in, so a team member does them, in order. Payments are Adi's (marked Adi pays): the team member stops there, Adi pays, and the team member carries on.

Target: every Workspace paid by Monday 9 November 2026 (Day 0). Then the first cold invites go out 3 December and the first full 100,000 week is the 18 February 2027 webinar (CAPACITY_PLAN.md).

Files you need open: DOMAIN_SHORTLIST.md (names and which Workspace each goes to), DNS_RECORDS.md (records to paste), sender-roster.csv (the 72 users).

Step 0. Today: Can the Older Senders Carry 12 November?

  1. Open https://usetantra.com, Accounts, and search each of: strategywebinars, visionarywebinars, seminarvision, nextgenwebinars, signonline.
  2. For each, note whether it shows Connected and the date its Workspace was created.
  3. Tell the build agent the count that is Connected and past day 60. If it is under five, the agent reruns the item 2 slicer so S01 matches (1,750 is the five-sender figure).

Step 1. The strategywebinars Workspace (W1): Domain, Plan, $100

Adi gives the team member the W1 admin login from ~/.config/tantra/keys/strategywebinars-workspace.env (Adi only; an agent never opens it).

  1. Open https://admin.google.com and sign in with that login. It signs in on a Google temporary domain (...appstempdomain.goog).
  2. Open https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/subscriptions. If the subscription says Trial, click it, then End trial (or Set up billing). Pick Business Starter, Flexible Plan. Adi pays if Google asks for a card.
  3. Open https://admin.google.com/ac/domains/manage. If strategywebinars.com is not listed: click Add a domain, type strategywebinars.com in Domain name, choose Secondary domain, click Add domain and start verification. Copy the TXT value Google shows.
  4. strategywebinars.com's DNS is at Cloudflare. Open https://dash.cloudflare.com, click strategywebinars.com, DNS, Records, Add record: Type TXT, Name @, Content the value from 1.3, Save. Back in Google, click Verify my domain.
  5. On https://admin.google.com/ac/domains/manage, next to strategywebinars.com click Make primary. If Google asks to set up Gmail MX first: add MX @ SMTP.GOOGLE.COM priority 1 at Cloudflare the same way.
  6. Rename the admin off the temporary domain: https://admin.google.com/ac/users, click the admin user, User information, Primary email, choose @strategywebinars.com, Save. Adi updates the login file himself.
  7. Adi pays $100: https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/accounts, click the payment account, Make a payment, Amount 100 (USD), Make a payment. The 60-day clock starts that day.

Tell the build agent: the W1 payment date.

Step 2. Register the 16 Domains at iFreeDomains

  1. Open https://ifreedomains.com/register.php. Fill First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Address, City, State, Postcode, Country with the company's real details (Adi supplies them; the registry requires them), set a Password, click Register.
  2. Open https://ifreedomains.com/cart.php?a=add&domain=register. Type the first name in DOMAIN_SHORTLIST.md (northgatesessions.com), click Check, Add to Cart on the $0.00 result, Checkout, Complete Order. If the cart asks for any payment, stop: that is an Adi pays decision.
  3. Click the ICANN registrant verification email. The registry emails the registrant address once per registrant contact. Check spam. It must be clicked within 15 days, or the registry suspends every domain on that contact.
  4. Open https://lookup.icann.org, type northgatesessions.com, click Lookup. It must show a registrar and today's date. If not, stop and tell the build agent (the fallback is Adi buying the names at Cloudflare).
  5. Repeat 2.2 for the other 11 sending domains and the 4 spares. If a name is taken or priced as "Special", skip it, take the next spare and tell the build agent which.
  6. For each domain: https://ifreedomains.com/clientarea.php?action=domains, Manage Domain, Management Tools, Nameservers, choose Use default nameservers, Change Nameservers.
  7. Same domain, Manage iFreeDomains DNS: add the MX, SPF and DMARC lines from its block in DNS_RECORDS.md (Name @ means leave Name blank; MX Priority 1). The two FILL lines come in steps 3, 4 and 6. Spares get their records only when they are brought in.
  8. Once the funnel exists, same page: Management Tools, URL Forwarding, paste the registration page (domains.funnel_domain in deliverables/_shared/funnel-config.json, still FILL), choose Redirect (301), Set URL.

Step 3. Create Workspaces W2, W3 and W4

Three times: W2 on ridgewayinsights.com, W3 on larkspurinsights.com, W4 on juniperhillinsights.com.

  1. Open https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome. Business name: the company's legal name (as on W1's billing). Number of employees: 2 to 9. Region: the company's country. Next.
  2. Contact details and current email, Next.
  3. Does your business have a domain?: Yes, I have one I can use, type the primary domain, Next, then Next on Use this domain to set up the account.
  4. How you'll sign in: username admin, a new password (in the team password manager, never in this repo or a chat), Agree and continue.
  5. Pick Business Starter. Adi pays: Adi adds the card and finishes checkout.
  6. Google shows a TXT verification value: add it at iFreeDomains (Manage iFreeDomains DNS, TXT, Name blank, Value as shown), then click Verify in Google.
  7. End the trial: https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/subscriptions, click the subscription, End trial (or Set up billing), pick Business Starter, Flexible Plan. A trial keeps Google's lowest limits however much is prepaid.
  8. Adi pays $100: https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/accounts, the payment account, Make a payment, Amount 100, Make a payment.

Tell the build agent the payment date of each Workspace. It writes it into warmup_start_day0 and re-dates the plan if it is not 9 November.

Step 4. Add the Other Two Domains to Each Workspace

Signed in to the right Workspace (the Workspace column of DOMAIN_SHORTLIST.md; W1 gets all three of its sending domains here):

  1. https://admin.google.com/ac/domains/manage, Add a domain, type the domain in Domain name, choose Secondary domain, Add domain and start verification.
  2. Paste the TXT value Google shows into that domain's DNS at iFreeDomains (first line of its block in DNS_RECORDS.md), then Verify my domain. If it does not show Verified, wait 30 minutes and click again.
  3. If Google shows Activate Gmail, click it: the MX record is already in place.

Step 5. DMARC Alias per Domain

https://admin.google.com/ac/users, click the admin user (in W1, after step 1.6), User information, Alternate email addresses (email alias), Add an alternate email, type dmarc, pick the domain, Save. One alias for each of the Workspace's 3 sending domains.

Step 6. DKIM for Every Sending Domain

  1. https://admin.google.com/ac/apps/gmail/authenticateemail.
  2. Pick the domain in Selected domain, Generate new record, keep DKIM key bit length 2048 and Prefix selector google, Generate.
  3. Copy the TXT record value into that domain's DNS at iFreeDomains: TXT, Name google._domainkey, Value as copied.
  4. After 30 minutes, same page, Start authentication. If it errors, wait and click again.

Step 7. Create the 72 Senders

18 per Workspace, already named in sender-roster.csv (columns domain, mailbox_name, display_name, email).

  1. https://admin.google.com/ac/users, Bulk update users, Download blank CSV template.
  2. One row per sender of that Workspace: First Name, Last Name (from display_name), Email Address (email), Password (new, in the team password manager), Org Unit Path /.
  3. Attach CSV file, pick the file, Upload. Google emails the admin when the users exist. Each new seat bills to Adi's card on the Flexible plan.
  4. Put the creation date in the created column.

Step 8. Connect the Senders to Tantra

One service account per sender (google-workspace.md), one Google Cloud project per Workspace (service_account_project in the roster).

  1. Fastest: the onboarding centre's Build my service accounts button, signed in once as each Workspace's super admin.
  2. By hand: https://console.cloud.google.com, create the project named in the roster, enable Gmail API and Google Calendar API, then IAM and Admin, Service Accounts, Create service account, open it, Keys, Add key, Create new key, JSON. Upload each file at https://usetantra.com, Connectors, Google Service Accounts, Upload Service Accounts, Upload. Never put a key file in this repo or a chat.
  3. Authorize each: https://admin.google.com/ac/owl/domainwidedelegation, Add new, paste the numeric Client ID, paste this in OAuth scopes, Authorize:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify
  1. https://usetantra.com, Accounts, Verify and Add Accounts, paste the Workspace's 18 sender addresses (one per line), Verify & Add. Each must show Connected; "unauthorized_client" means the email was pasted instead of the numeric Client ID.
  2. Mark tantra_service_account_connected yes in the roster.

Step 9. Warm-Up Weeks, Then the Daily Limit Every Thursday

  1. 12 November to 2 December: no cold invites. Weeks of 12 and 19 November: each sender accepts two or three internal calendar events a day from a teammate in the same Workspace and joins one short Google Meet. Week of 26 November: each sender invites 3 to 5 known contacts a day (partners, clients, the team's own work addresses) who accept.
  2. Every Thursday from 3 December: in https://usetantra.com, Accounts, open each sender and set its daily invite limit to the roster's number for that week's webinar (daily_cap_2026-12-10 and on): 10, then 20, 50, 75, 100, 150, and 200 from 11 February. Set each campaign to send all 7 days. Raise a step only while every sender in that Workspace shows Connected; otherwise keep last week's number. With Adi's written OK, the build agent can set the limits through the Tantra API instead (PATCH /accounts/<email> with maxPerDay).

CAPACITY_PLAN.md

Capacity Plan: 100,000 Calendar Invites a Week

The Short Answer

72 sender accounts on 12 domains (6 mailboxes each), in 4 Google Workspaces, send 100,800 invites a week at 200 a sender a day, 7 days a week. They cannot do it on day one. If the team starts setup now and every Workspace has its $100 paid by Monday 9 November 2026, the first full 100,000 week is the one for the Thursday 18 February 2027 webinar (sent 11 to 17 February). Setup is a manual job for the team (ADI_STEPS.md); payments are Adi's.

The Maths

Invites needed a week                  = 100,000
Send days a week                       = 7
Invites a day                          = 100,000 / 7               = 14,286
Invites a sender a day (full volume)   = 200
Senders needed                         = 14,286 / 200              = 71.4  -> 72
Domains                                = 72 / 6 mailboxes a domain = 12
Capacity a week                        = 72 x 200 x 7              = 100,800
Invites a domain a week                = 6 x 200 x 7               = 8,400
Workspaces                             = 4 (W1 strategywebinars + 3 new), 3 domains and 18 senders each

Why 4 Workspaces: Google suspends a Workspace, not a user. With 4, one suspension stops a quarter of the volume (25,200 a week); the other three keep sending. Each Workspace uses one Google Cloud project for its 18 service accounts, under the default of 100 per project.

Where 200 a Day Sits Against Our Own Rules

Source Says
context/from-workshop-project/03-calendar-infra/calendar-invites.md, Limits 20 a sender a day while the Workspace is young, 50 after day 60, never above 200
context/past-session-decisions.md (2026-10-02) $100 prepay and a 60-day warm-up before volume
Tantra's blog, ~/tantra-redesign/app/content/blog/cold-google-calendar-outreach-2026-limits-warmup.mdx First cold invite around day 21; 25 to 50 a day after day 90; 100 to 150 an "absolute ceiling" with elevated risk above it

200 a day is Adi's number and the playbook's hard ceiling; it is above the blog's ceiling. So the ramp below holds the playbook (20 until day 60, 50 from day 60) and climbs to 200 in steps, one step a week, only while senders stay healthy. The load per domain is also high: the senders behind the master list ran about 1,440 invites a domain a week (references/cold-calendar-exports-E150-E162-stats.md); this plan runs 8,400, 5.8 times that. That is why there are 4 spare domains and a stop rule.
