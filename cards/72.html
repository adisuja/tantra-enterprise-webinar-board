PRD: The Tantra 100K MRR Club (offer, placement, course, membership)

Card 72 on the board. Written 13 November 2026. Method: the gstack planning sequence (office hours, CEO review, engineering review, design review, then QA and ship), one section each below.

1. Office Hours: The Problem, Sharpened

Who is this for? An owner-led B2B business (coaching, consulting, agencies, SaaS, training) doing somewhere between $10K and $100K a month, with a team member who can build, and an offer that already sells. They watched the webinar, believed the system, and either cannot fund a done for you Enterprise build or would rather own it.

What do they do today? They buy the $99 RevOps OS or start a Tantra Pro trial, connect one mailbox, then stall. The stall points are known from our own builds: domain and mailbox setup, Google delegation, wiring payments into the CRM, and not knowing which number to fix once traffic starts.

What is the narrowest thing that works? A member with a live, tested funnel and traffic running by day 7, and one experiment a week after that. Not a course they watch. A build they finish, with someone checking it.

What does success look like at 30 days? Build Score 100 by Day 2 or 3. Traffic on at least three channels by day 7. Cold email live by day 14. At least one booked sales call from the funnel and the first experiment decided by day 30. Closed revenue tracked in the CRM from the payment webhook, not from a spreadsheet.

What we will not claim. No member result exists yet. Until one does, every page and message promises the build, the coaching and the timeline, never a revenue figure.

3. Engineering Review: Architecture and Data

Full spec in BUILD_SYSTEM.md. Key points:

  • Hub and spokes. The Funnel OS chat is the hub. Spokes: Tantra app, Tantra CRM, Content OS, payment provider, Cloudflare, analytics. Every spoke is reached through an API key or MCP connection the member creates in V03.
  • One source of truth for money. A sale exists only when the signed payment webhook reaches the Tantra CRM. Thank-you page visits never count.
  • Membership provisioning. Approved applicant pays through a Dodo link: $5,999 once for 6 months of access, or a subscription at $1,999 for the first month then $999 a month. subscription.active and payment.succeeded reach the CRM, which moves the deal to Won (club) and calls the Tantra app to grant Academy access, a Tantra Pro seat and a Content OS workspace. Sequence CL-M starts. Failure mode: if the grant call fails, the CRM opens a task for the ops owner within 5 minutes and the member still gets the welcome email.
  • CRM fields added: contact readiness_score, readiness_route, build_score, funnel_worst_step, club_cohort; deal plan_offer = club_100k (exists), os_credit_applied.
  • Pipeline: the existing "Tantra Plans (webinar offer)" pipeline keeps the "100K MRR Club application" stage; add "Club approved, unpaid", "Club member (paid)", "Club churned".
  • Edge cases: applicant pays after the webinar price deadline (the price holds for approved applicants for 7 days); RevOps OS buyer applies after the 7 day credit (no credit); a member's mailboxes are not warm by day 14 (course V29 says what to run meanwhile); a member's payment provider is not verified by Day 2 (build in test mode, swap to live in V33).

PLACEMENT_STRATEGY.md

Where the 100K MRR Club Shows Up, and Why There

The club sits in the middle of the ladder. Above it are the done for you clubs, the $5M ARR Club and the $10M ARR Club (Adi 13 November: there is no separate Tantra Enterprise plan any more). Below it are Tantra Pro ($74.97 a month for 3 months on the webinar code, then $99) and the Tantra RevOps OS ($99, one payment). Today the club is reachable from three places only: the plans page, its own landing page and Bonus 04 on the bonus hub. Nobody who says "too expensive" to Enterprise, and nobody who buys the $99 OS or starts a Pro trial, is ever shown it.

So the strategy is one sentence: put the club wherever a person has just shown they want to build it themselves, or has just told us Enterprise is out of reach. Those are the two moments a coached build ($5,999 for 6 months, or $1,999 then $999 a month) makes the most sense to them.

4. The Funnel Readiness Score (lead scoring and routing)

One score routes everyone to the right offer, on the /score/ page, on the club booking form and on the call.

Question group Points
Current monthly revenue band (under $10K, $10K to $50K, $50K to $250K, above) 0 to 25
Who would build it (me, my team, an agency, nobody yet) 0 to 20
Offer proven (has paying clients, price above $2K) 0 to 15
A list or audience exists (contacts, followers, past clients) 0 to 15
Hours a week they can give the build 0 to 15
Budget for the next 12 months 0 to 10

Routing: score below 40 and budget under $5K goes to Tantra Pro. Score 40 to 79, or "my team builds it", goes to the club. Score 80 and above with budget of $10K or more goes to Enterprise. The CRM stores readiness_score, readiness_route and the six answers on the contact, and the closer sees them before the call.

The 100K MRR Club Course: 34 Videos in 30 Days

Delivered inside the Tantra Academy in the Tantra app (usetantra.com). Every video is a screen recording with plain white slides (black text, Inter), voiced by Adi's cloned voice. Scripts: scripts/V01 to scripts/V34. Slides: slides/.

How We Got to 34 (the analysis)

We counted every build step in three sources and grouped them by how much screen time each one takes to show:

Source What it holds Build steps found
Current playbook (Bonus 01, deliverables/33-bonus-books/content/playbook/) 4 phases, 6 stages, 8 funnel types, about 45 tasks, 24 element prompts. Tool agnostic: names no Tantra product 33
Previous playbook (context/from-tantra-content/sources/workshop/playbook.html) The Tantra stack version: Day 0 to Day 3, the funnel equations, the weekly loop, 17 extra funnels 39
Onboarding centre (138 cards, Day 0 to Day 3, 04-workshop-curriculum.md) The click by click setup for every tool 41 after merging

Merged and de-duplicated, that is 41 build steps from an empty folder to money in the bank. Then the grouping rules:

  1. Small steps that share one screen become one video. Offer, method and value equation all happen in the same Funnel OS chat, so they are one video (V04).
  2. Large steps get their own video. Connecting mailboxes through Google delegation is the step most people stall on, so it is a video by itself (V09).
  3. A system that spans channels compresses into a short series. The Content OS is three videos (set up, publish, turn posts into registrations), not one per channel.
  4. Anything a member does every day or every week gets its own video, because they will rewatch it: the dashboard, the worst step, experiments, the daily routine.
  5. Things we do not teach: the 17 extra funnel types (one video covers choosing a funnel), the old Cal.com, GoHighLevel and Metricool steps (replaced by the Tantra CRM and the Content OS), WhatsApp group posting tools that break WhatsApp's terms.

Result: 41 steps become 34 videos, about 5 hours 30 minutes in total. No video runs over 15 minutes.

The Two Scores Members See

Build Score (out of 100, weeks 1 and 2). Every video ends with a short "Done when" checklist. Each finished item adds points. Funnel Score (weeks 2 to 4). The six stage rates from the dashboard (visit, sign-up, show-up, booking, close, rebooking), each shown against the member's own previous week, never against another business. The weekly call starts with the lowest one.
