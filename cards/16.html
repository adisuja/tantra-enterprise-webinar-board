The 45-slide build dropped, folded and merged about 20 reference moves; Adi rejected that. This map is the contract for the rebuild: every distinct slide state of the reference recording, in order, and the Tantra slide or tab that mirrors it. How the recording was walked. Frames every 12 seconds at 1280 wide (.work/qs-frames-hd/tNNNN.jpg at the repo root, frame N is (N minus 1) times 12 seconds), plus a 3-second pass (deliverables/16-deck/.work/qs-frames-3s/, 1,603 frames, change-point scanned) to catch states shorter than 12 seconds; those got HD grabs named sMMSS.jpg for the second they were taken, or the 3-second frame upscaled as fNNNN.jpg ((N minus 1) times 3 seconds). The first pass missed seven short states (an inflection statement at 3:00, the self-proof slide at 5:09, two market tabs, one opt-in tab, the idle sending engine and the "into the brain" tab); the fidelity critic found them and they are in. Both folders are gitignored. The left camera tile is the presenter, not the slide. From 45:12 to 45:48 the recording shows only cameras (the second presenter joining); from 46:00 to the end (80:08) one final slide is held, with no further change (checked frame by frame). Transcript: research/qs-webinar-transcript.srt.

Structure only. No wording, names, numbers or case studies from the reference appear in the deck: a 6-word overlap check of build/spec.mjs and deck/index.html against the srt returns 0 shared runs. The srt cannot see slide text that was never spoken, so every headline, caption and eyebrow was also read against the frames; the near-copies the critic found ("one click", "every number that matters", and eyebrows such as "Quick fit check") were rewritten. Generic UI chrome (Previous tab, Next tab, End of tabs) is kept on purpose: it is the visual element.

Run of Show: the 60-Minute Tantra Enterprise Webinar

45 minutes of teaching and invitation, then 15 minutes of Q and A. One deck: deck/index.html (74 slides, 110 states with tabs), rebuilt 2026-11-05 to follow the reference recording slide by slide and at about its minute (REFERENCE_MAP.md). The offer lands at minute 20, before the minute-25 early-leaver cutoff. Polls, chat lines and offer cues word for word: ../21-engagement/ENGAGEMENT_PLAN.md.

People. Adi hosts alone: the only person on camera and the only voice. There is no co-host. The moderator works behind the scenes, never on camera or mic (in WebinarJam Basic they take the second presenter seat with the camera off, because Basic has no Moderator role): launches polls and offers, posts chat lines, answers chat, sends Adi the question list in private chat. Adi confirms who moderates before each session.

Presenter keys. Right arrow or Space: next (on a tabbed slide it steps through the tabs first; the footer shows the tab counter and progress bar). N: speaker notes (each opens with the planned minute and the chat prompt). G or O: every slide. F: full screen. ?notes=1 on the URL opens with notes.

Pace. Many slides last 10 to 20 seconds, as in the reference: one line, then Right arrow. The big words (slides 16, 18, 19), the "three parts" slides (32, 46, 60) and the centred questions (22, 39, 42, 49, 54, 62) are five to fifteen seconds each.

Clock rules. Minute 20 is fixed: if slide 34 (the client quote) is not up by minute 19, skip tabs 3 and 5 of slide 28 and tab 2 of slide 30 so the offer slide (35) lands by minute 20 and always before minute 25. If the recap (slide 63) is not up by minute 34, skip tabs 2 to 4 of slide 63 and go straight to the QR on slide 64. Protect the first look (minutes 20 to 22), the full offer (minutes 36 to 45) and the full 15 minutes of Q and A.

The Q and A Plan (minutes 45 to 60)

  1. Order. Poll 4 D-voters first (they told you they have a question before buying), then "will this work for my industry", then how the build works, then price and terms, then everything else.
  2. Every answer ends with the next step, one sentence: "That's exactly what the call is for: the link is pinned."
  3. Industry fit. Answer honestly. Works best: B2B, a high-trust offer sold on a call, buyers at a desk. Harder: buyers away from a desk, low-price impulse products. If it's a poor fit, say so; that makes every other yes believable.
  4. Price. $9,999, said on slide 35 (minute 20) and again on slide 69 (minute 39). Repeat it only if asked. No discount, no deadline, no guarantee unless Adi has approved one in funnel-config.json.
  5. Proof questions. Only PROOF_PACK numbers: Growth Idea and FintechCircle measured figures, the four host lines, verbatim quotes. Anything else: "I'll send you the details after the call."
  6. Hand-off. Questions the moderator can answer in chat (links, replay, time zone) never reach Adi. The moderator never speaks on the session.
  7. After booking. If asked what happens next: the Evaluation Call, a written proposal within one business day, then a Proposal Review call with the same closer, where we decide together. A missed call is not rescheduled.
  8. Close. At minute 58, final call to action whatever the queue, then keep answering for whoever stays.
