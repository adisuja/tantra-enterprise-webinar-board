Deviations, and Why

  • Order: the calendar invite (reference step 2) sits in our step 1, because for Tantra the invite is how the room fills; our step 2 is show-up (the reference's reminder slide from its step 3), our step 3 is conversion to booked calls. The third proof card (reference "ceiling") comes before the recap, so each step closes on proof.
  • Polls: the reference has none. Our four polls keep the CRM data path (item 21) and use the reference's callout layout with a poll card. Poll 1 sits on the qualifier slide, replacing its chart card; the chart moved to slide 12.
  • Dropped for the 45-slide budget: about 20 reference beats (listed above as dropped or folded). Every dropped beat is either folded into a neighbour's notes or tabs, or has no honest equivalent (data price cards, client video, live-run videos, the 12-month revenue projection).
  • Honest replacements: monograms for client photos, tracker tables for chat screenshots, generic icons for AI brand logos, a strip line where the reference has a client quote. The guarantee card becomes the first step (the call and the proposal) and the monthly-limit line is left off; both FILLs live in the speaker notes only. The bonus values stay off; the bonus cards are marked proposed.
  • Headline case: sentence case with a full stop, as in the reference. Adi decided 2026-11-05: formatting follows the reference, so the deck keeps sentence case (this overrides AGENT_RULES rule 2's Title Case for the deck).
  • Early offer (Adi, 2026-11-05): the early-leaver cutoff stays at minute 25, so the offer moved ahead of it. Slide 23 (minute 20) shows what Tantra Enterprise is, who it is for, the price and the next step; slide 24 (minute 22) is the QR. To keep 45 slides and 45 minutes, the by-hand slide became tab 4 of slide 20, and the problem and step 1 run one minute shorter each (poll 2 at 13, the engine at 14, step 1 from 15). The full offer, journey, cost, comparison and close stay at minutes 33 to 45.
  • No co-host: Adi hosts alone. The moderator is behind the scenes only and never speaks; the Q and A slide's speaker line and notes say so.
