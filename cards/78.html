The $5M and $10M ARR Clubs: Implementation and Coaching Structure (draft for Adi)

Adi, 13 November 2026: the done for you offers are the $5M ARR Club and the $10M ARR Club. They are groups by revenue stage, not entry rules: the $5M ARR Club is for companies past roughly $0.5M to $1M ARR, and the $10M ARR Club for companies further on. Unlike the 100K MRR Club, there are no self-paced members here. Each client gets an implementation and coaching structure that has to be built, communicated, presented and planned. This page is that structure, as a first draft for Adi to change.

The 90 Days

Phase Weeks Outcome Lead coach
0. Onboarding Week 0 Contract signed, kickoff call, access to the client's accounts (domains, Google Workspace, LinkedIn, payments), the shared WhatsApp group, the onboarding deck presented Conor
1. Audit and plan Week 1 Current funnel and numbers audited; offer, buyer, list and funnel decided; the 90 day plan presented and signed off Adi
2. Build Weeks 2 to 3 The six stages built: traffic engine, landing and registration, nurture, conversion step, revenue engine, long-term nurture. Pages live, CRM wired, payments live, scripts written Bhaskar (systems), Conor (CRM), Scott (scripts)
3. Launch Week 4 Test run passed, traffic on, first sales calls with Scott reviewing recordings Scott, Conor
4. Optimise Weeks 5 to 12 Worst step fixed every week, one experiment a week, monthly scale plan, call coaching Phase owner each week, Adi monthly

What the Client Sees (to build)

  1. Onboarding deck (presented on the kickoff call): the team and their roles, the 90 days, the weekly rhythm, what we need from them, how the 10X ROI Guarantee is measured.
  2. The 90 day plan (presented in week 1): one page per phase with dates, owners and the numbers we aim to move.
  3. The weekly update template (email and WhatsApp).
  4. A client board: the same card format as this board, one per client, with every task, link and decision.

In the CRM

  • The pipeline after "Won": Onboarding, Audit, Build, Launch, Optimise, Renewal.
  • The weekly one to one call as a coaching series per client (card 77).
  • A sequence for onboarding steps and a reminder before each call.
