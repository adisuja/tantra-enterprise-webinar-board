Card 40: launch audit (8 November 2026)

What has to be true before the first live session, and before any traffic is sent. It covers everything built in the last 24 hours (registration page, survey, upsell, checkout, confirmation, calculator, bonuses, WhatsApp, funnel map, Tantra CRM) and the cards around them. Every gap has a card; the board now lists cards by priority: launch blockers first, finished cards at the bottom.

Verdict

The pages, bonuses, checkout and copy are built. The funnel cannot take a real registration yet: nothing is deployed to a funnel domain, the register Worker is not deployed, WebinarJam is not set up, no CRM receives the funnel's events, and nothing sends the 34 messages. The RevOps OS that the upsell sells does not exist as a product yet.

Date risk. The first session is Thursday 12 November at 10:00 UK, four days away. Calendar invites need the pages live first (card 30), and the WhatsApp group scheduler will post the first reminder for 12 November into the community on Wednesday 11 November whatever happens. Decide the date now (card 30): keep 12 November, or make it an internal dry run and go live on 19 November.

Critical path

  2. Funnel domain and deploy (card 46), WebinarJam account and event (card 17), register Worker deployed.
  3. CRM of record chosen and the funnel events wired into it (card 41).

  6. Survey and upsell videos (card 48), tracking and consent (card 47).

Risks worth knowing

  • Sending the upsell before the OS exists: buyers get only a receipt. Either build it (card 44) or hide the Yes button until it is ready.
  • WhatsApp without an explicit opt-in tick box can get the sender number flagged by Meta (card 47 decision).
  • The Twilio API key secret pasted in chat on 8 November still needs rotating (card 43).
  • Tantra CRM code lives in Usetantra repos on branch feature/crm-plivo-dialer; agents never push there, so the funnel endpoint in card 41 is applied by Adi or Bharath.
  • The community group scheduler posts to real members on a schedule; a moved date must move it too.
