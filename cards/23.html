README.md

Item 23: CRM Sheet and Pipeline Integrations

One Google Sheet is the CRM. One Apps Script web app writes to it. Every form, booking, webinar export and reply posts an event; the script moves the deal and schedules the next message. Nobody drags a deal by hand.

The sheet (owner only): https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1d_AhlTv5Tr0O2vPL2E448SUbQZP7q04tpSGTC7pSv1Q/edit (id in SHEET_ID.md).

How It Works

Event in What the CRM does
webinar.registered, attended, no_show, poll_answered, cta_clicked Contact upsert (incl. whatsapp, call and newsletter opt-ins); Webinar row per email per date with segment, track, retarget_until (date + 42 days for early leavers and no-shows) and one poll_<id> column per poll. An offer click with no deal opens 1 New Lead and a 5-minute hot-lead task
booking.created, rescheduled, cancelled (or raw Cal.com BOOKING_*) Deal to 2 Call Booked with the round-robin host as closer; Seq. A, B and the setter, prep and outcome tasks; closer alert email. Reschedule replaces the reminders. Cancel: 1 New Lead and Seq. E
call.confirmed, showed, no_show, not_ready Stages 3, 4a, 4b; proposal task; Seq. C (rebook steps behind seq_c_rebook); Seq. F
proposal.sent, deal.won, deal.lost Stages 5, 7, 8 (Timing goes to 9 Nurture); Seq. D (proposal with the closer's Call 2 link); welcome, handover and testimonial tasks
Call 2: booking.* and call.showed / call.no_show with data.call: 2 (raw Cal.com: event type slug proposal-review, Lists setting calcom_call2_slug) Sets call2_date and call2_status (Booked, Rescheduled, Showed, No-Show); the deal stays in 5 Proposal Sent (from 4a, 6 or 9 it moves to 5), never back to 2 Call Booked; the Call 1 date and closer are kept. Seq. D stops; Seq. R (reminders on Seq. B timing), RN (no-show, seq_c_rebook switch), RC (cancelled) or RS (after Call 2, Follow-Up on day 7). Outcomes from the sheet: Tantra CRM, Call 2 Showed or Call 2 No-Show (a plain Showed on a booked Call 2 is read as Call 2)
sequence.sent Marks the step sent and stores the Vodex outcome
contact.quick_reply (YES, CAL, CANT) Logged on Sequences, stops nothing. CANT sets track to replay on the Webinar row. YES on a deal at 2 Call Booked confirms the call
contact.replied, contact.opted_out Stop every chasing sequence and raise a reply task; STOP sets Do Not Contact and stops everything

The runner (every 5 minutes) sends each due step only if the deal is still in a stage the sequence allows, the lead has not replied, the contact is not Do Not Contact, and, for WhatsApp, it is 08:00 to 20:00 in the lead's timezone. The CRM's own clock is UK time: the Lists setting default_timezone is Europe/London (webinar.timezone), used when a lead has no timezone and for the closer's side of every alert, and Set up sets the spreadsheet's timezone (File, Settings) to it so every timestamp column reads in UK time, not the owner account's IST. On a sheet seeded earlier, Set up also replaces the old America/New_York placeholder. Time-based moves (No-Show to Nurture at day 7, Proposal Sent to Follow-Up at day 7, Follow-Up to Nurture at day 14) are system steps. A message holding a FILL_ value is marked blocked, never sent.

Sending. Default dispatch_mode is queue: due WhatsApps and emails wait in Sequences as due and the closer gets the exact text by email to send from Tantra, then clicks Tantra CRM, Mark selected Sequences row sent or done. tantra mode sends through the Tantra API as single-step campaigns; field names come from the Tantra API reference (simulator verified), so test it once on Adi's own number before switching.

Reps work from the menu. Tantra CRM menu on the sheet: Confirmed, Showed, No-Show, Not ready, Proposal sent, Call 2 Showed, Call 2 No-Show, Closed Won, Closed Lost, Lead replied, Lead opted out (each on the selected Pipeline row), Mark selected Sequences row sent or done, Import WebinarJam attendance, Run follow-ups now, Set up.

Adi Steps (Item 23)

The sheet already exists in your Drive. Deploying the script is yours because it runs as you.

  1. Open https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1d_AhlTv5Tr0O2vPL2E448SUbQZP7q04tpSGTC7pSv1Q/edit
  2. Top menu Extensions, then Apps Script. A new tab opens with a file called Code.gs.
  3. Click inside Code.gs, select all (Cmd+A), delete, then paste the whole of Code.gs: open (in this card) (live once the lead session has pushed), click Raw, Cmd+A, Cmd+C. Click the Save (disk) icon. Name the project Tantra CRM if asked.
  4. Go back to the sheet tab and reload it. A Tantra CRM menu appears after a few seconds. Click Tantra CRM, Set up (run once). Google asks for permission: Continue, pick your growthclub.org account, Advanced, Go to Tantra CRM (unsafe) (it is your own script), Allow. Click Set up (run once) again. A box shows the shared secret: copy it somewhere private (it also lives in Apps Script, Project Settings, Script Properties, SHARED_SECRET).
  5. In the Apps Script tab: Deploy, New deployment, the gear next to "Select type", Web app. Description: Tantra CRM v1. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy, then Authorize access if asked. Copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec). Click Done.
  6. Send me the Web app URL (not the secret). It goes into funnel-config.json integrations.crm_webhook_url. Every sender then posts to <URL>?k=<secret>; you paste the ?k= version only into Cal.com (item 19, section 6) and Tantra (step 8), never into the repo.
  7. On the Lists tab, column closer_email and closer_whatsapp: Conor's and Scott's work emails (the ones on Cal.com) and WhatsApp numbers. Column value: booking_url (the Cal.com round-robin link), onboarding_link, and due_diligence_url once the funnel domain exists. Messages that need a missing value wait as blocked.
  8. Replies and STOP from Tantra: in Tantra (https://usetantra.com), Connectors, Webhooks (exact menu label unverified), add a webhook with URL <Web app URL>?k=<secret> and turn on email replied, email unsubscribed and WhatsApp message replied. After: a WhatsApp YES confirms a booked call, STOP sets Do Not Contact.
  9. After each webinar: in WebinarJam export the attendee list as CSV; in the sheet File, Import, Upload, choose the CSV, Insert new sheet(s), Import data; rename the new tab to WJ Import; then Tantra CRM, Import WebinarJam attendance, type the webinar date (YYYY-MM-DD). After: the Webinar tab shows attended, stayed_to_offer, early_leaver and no_show with retarget dates; delete the WJ Import tab.

After any code change: Apps Script, Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil icon, Version New version, Deploy. The URL stays the same.

Call 2 update (2026-11-05) on the live sheet. The live sheet was not re-imported: create_gsheet.py --update replaces the whole file with the template, which would wipe any rows or Lists values already typed, so it cannot add columns safely. Instead, after pasting the new Code.gs (step 3) and deploying a new version, open the sheet and click Tantra CRM, Set up (run once) again. It appends call2_date, call2_status, call2_cal_uid and call2_join_link to the end of the Pipeline header, closer_call2_url to the Lists header and the calcom_call2_slug setting (value proposal-review) under the other settings, and changes nothing else. Then type each closer's Proposal Review link (item 19, section 8) into closer_call2_url on their row. Until a closer's link is there, the proposal email and every Call 2 message for that closer wait as blocked. The call2_status dropdown and the five Call 2 rows on the Summary tab come only with a fresh import of the template; the CRM works without them.

Notes for the Lead Session (Contract)

  • The sheet has a Summary tab (formulas only) that CRM_CONTRACT.md does not list. Add it to the tab table.
  • Sequences rows also carry rep tasks (channel task), runner stage moves (channel system), quick replies (sequence QUICK_REPLY, status received) and the closer's reply tasks (sequence TASK). Events has a kind column: post for POSTs, runner for stage moves the runner made.
  • Webinar has a track column (live or replay) for contact.quick_reply CANT; item 15b and 22 can read it.
  • Cal.com MEETING_ENDED (flat payload, fires at every booking's end time) and a no-show unmark have no contract event: each is logged and raises a closer task, never a stage move.
  • Pages that post from the browser would expose ?k=. Registration and form posts should go through a server or the WebinarJam integration, not straight from page JavaScript.
  • Revenue bands and invest_12m (yes/no) on the Lists tab and the Cal.com questions match the registration page. Poll 1 (item 21) still uses other bands: align it to the page.

  • Call 2 (CRM_CONTRACT "Call 2"): the contract names call2_date and call2_status. The code also writes call2_cal_uid (matches a Cal.com no-show to Call 2) and call2_join_link (the Meet link in Call 2 reminders). Add both to the contract.
