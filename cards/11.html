Item 11: Cold Email Templates

Three cold email templates inviting Enterprise-fit founders to the weekly webinar, sent from Tantra. Each: 3 subject lines, an AI first line, the body and 2 follow-ups.

Claim to verify: none in these emails. They name the session in plain words and never print {{webinar.title}}, so "400+ founders" and "$100K to $300K" (no source yet, PROOF_PACK.md section 2) appear only on the registration page. All proof is from PROOF_PACK.md: worked with 250+ brands; Growth Idea (as "a UK coaching company"): 30 then 1,990 registrations, 98,000 of 99,859 reached by calendar invite; FintechCircle (as "a London fintech network"): 10,000 to 30,000 outreach, 17 calls in episodes 1 to 3; the offer's fit (wants it built with them).

{{company}} appears only inside the AI line, because the list's company is blank for personal email and rough when guessed from a domain. The tier 2 line is written with the company name filled in at generation, and only when a company is known; a blank company drops to tier 3, so no email ever prints a gap. If a body ever needs the tag itself, Tantra's form is {{company}} (or {{contact.company}}), never {{custom.company}}: an unknown tag renders blank (https://usetantra.com/help/templates-and-ai/merge-tags-and-spintax).

How To Load It Into Tantra (after Adi fills the config)

  1. python3 deliverables/11-cold-email/build.py --tantra (refuses while any value it needs is still FILL_).
  2. One Tantra sequence campaign per template, built as a draft (never /campaigns/create): "Enterprise webinar: cold email (A)", (B), (C), each on a third of the list; every contact carries ai_first_line in customFields.
  3. Senders: warmed cold email mailboxes only (never a calendar sender, never usetantra.com or growthclub.org). Monday to Friday, business hours in the prospect's time zone, 50 a day per mailbox once warm.
  4. Before launch, add one internal test contact (with a first name, a company and an ai_first_line in customFields) to each draft and send one test: {{firstName}} and {{custom.ai_first_line}} must both render, with no raw braces and no blank line where the AI line should be.
  5. Start the thread at least 8 days before a session so the last step lands before it.
  6. After a week, keep the template with the most registrations per 100 people reached (utm_content), and retire the weakest.

ai-first-line.md

The AI First Line: {{ai_first_line}}

Step 1 of every template opens with one generated sentence, written per prospect before import and stored in the lead's custom field ai_first_line. Follow-ups carry no AI line.

Inputs (from the master list, references/added-to-calendar-consolidated-2026-11-04.md)

Field Use
first_name Greeting only ("Hi {{first_name}},"). Already there when name_confidence is none, so the greeting reads "Hi there," with no extra logic
company Printed only if the same name appears in the homepage text (guessed names such as "Thekbbcompany" never print)
domain Source of the homepage text
name_confidence, email_type Pick the tier (below)
homepage_text Optional. First 1,500 characters of curl https://r.jina.ai/https://{{domain}}, fetched by the enrichment step

Tiers (take the first that applies)

Tier When What the line does
1 email_type business, homepage text readable Names what the company sells or who it serves, in the prospect's own homepage words
2 Business email, no usable homepage text, company found on the page or company_source is LinkedIn Fixed line: I'm writing to you as the person at {{company}} who most likely owns next month's pipeline.
3 name_confidence none with no homepage text, personal or role email, or anything else Fixed line: I'm writing to founders who sell B2B and want next month's revenue to be less of a guess.

Tier 3 is the fallback for name_confidence none: the greeting already says "Hi there," and the line names no person and no company.

The Prompt (tier 1)

You write the first sentence of a short cold email from Adi, founder of Tantra, to a founder.
The greeting ("Hi <name>,") is already written. The email body after your sentence invites them
to a live session on revenue systems. Your sentence only shows you looked at their business.

INPUT
company: {{company}}
domain: {{domain}}
homepage_text: {{homepage_text}}

WRITE one sentence, 10 to 22 words, that:
- names one specific thing from homepage_text: what they sell, who they serve, or how they describe themselves
- states it plainly, as an observation, in the second person ("you", "your team")
- reads like a person wrote it: contractions allowed, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no dashes

NEVER:
- invent anything: no numbers, clients, awards, funding, news or places unless they appear word for word in homepage_text
- flatter ("love what you're doing", "impressive", "great work")
- open with "I came across", "I noticed", "I hope", "Hope this finds you"
- mention AI, revenue, sales, webinars, sessions, Tantra or the person's name
- print the company name unless it appears in homepage_text exactly as given
- ask a question

If homepage_text is empty, unreadable, a parked or login page, or not about a business, output exactly: FALLBACK

OUTPUT the sentence only on line 1. Line 2: TIER: 1 (or FALLBACK on line 1 with no line 2).

On FALLBACK, apply tier 2 or tier 3 above.

Checks on Tier 1 Lines Before Import (reject the line and use tier 3 if any fails)

  1. 10 to 22 words, one sentence, ends with a full stop.
  2. No digit that is not in homepage_text.
  3. No "!", "?", em dash, en dash or emoji.
  4. None of: AI, revenue, webinar, session, Tantra, leverage, unlock, seamless, game-changer, supercharge, delve, robust, elevate.
  5. Read 20 random lines by eye before the first import of each weekly batch.

Examples (fictional companies, for tone only)

  • Tier 1: You help mid-sized logistics firms move freight across Europe without the paperwork piling up on your team.
  • Tier 2: I'm writing to you as the person at Northfield Advisory who most likely owns next month's pipeline.
  • Tier 3: I'm writing to founders who sell B2B and want next month's revenue to be less of a guess.
