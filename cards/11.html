README.md

Item 11: Cold Email Templates

Three cold email templates inviting Enterprise-fit founders to the weekly webinar, sent from Tantra. Each: 3 subject lines, a step 1 and 2 follow-ups. Every step names the real session: Thursday 12 November 2026, 10:00 UK time (11:00 CET), then every Thursday at the same time.

Personalisation (Adi 2026-11-05): merge tags only. The AI first line ({{ai_first_line}}, {{custom.ai_first_line}}) and its prompt file ai-first-line.md are retired and deleted. Step 1 now opens on a fixed line carrying {{company}}, and every step is a Tantra Predefined template, never AI-personalized.

Claim to verify: none in these emails. They name the session in plain words and never print {{webinar.title}}, so the title's $100K to $300K range (from Adi's brief) appears only on the registration page. All proof is from PROOF_PACK.md: worked with 400+ brands; Growth Idea (as "a UK coaching company"): 30 then 1,990 registrations, 98,000 of 99,859 reached by calendar invite; FintechCircle (as "a London fintech network"): 10,000 to 30,000 outreach, 17 calls in episodes 1 to 3; the offer's fit (wants it built with them).

delay_days is the wait since the previous step (Tantra's wait): steps on day 0, 3 and 7. Only step 1 has a subject; follow-ups go in the same thread. To test subject 2 or 3, build the same sequence again as its own campaign on its own list slice.

Fields

In the copy Filled by Value
{{firstName}} Tantra merge tag, per contact first name; import there when unknown, so it reads "Hi there,"
{{company}} Tantra merge tag, per contact the company only when the list confirmed it (company_source LinkedIn, or found on the homepage); import your company otherwise, so it reads "at your company"
Date, time, CET, day, host, minutes build.py from funnel-config.json Thursday 12 November, 10:00 UK time (11:00 CET); every Thursday
{{domains.funnel_domain}} in the link build.py, once set pending until the pages deploy

Tantra renders a tag with no value as blank and has no if/else (https://usetantra.com/help/templates-and-ai/merge-tags-and-spintax), so the fallbacks must be in the contact data: a blank company would print "at ." in step 1.

Each thread points at one session: for a later week, python3 build.py --tantra --date 2026-11-19 (a date that is not a Thursday is refused).

First send: the first Thursday after the funnel domain is live, with the 8 days a thread needs (threads start 8 days before the session). 12 November 2026 is already out of reach (its threads would have started 4 November). Run python3 build.py --tantra --date <that Thursday>; the default date is webinar.first_date, so never run it without --date.

Loading and Testing (manual, by the team, once the funnel domain is set)

  1. python3 deliverables/11-cold-email/build.py --tantra --date <session date> writes cold-email-tantra.csv.
  2. Import the contacts with firstName and company filled as in the table above (fallbacks included).
  3. Open https://usetantra.com/campaigns and create a cold email sequence campaign per template, as a draft: "Enterprise webinar : cold email (A)", (B), (C), each on a third of the list. Each step: mode Predefined template, paste subject and body from the CSV row, wait = delay_days.
  4. Senders: warmed cold email mailboxes only (never a calendar sender, never usetantra.com or growthclub.org). Monday to Friday, business hours in the prospect's time zone, 50 a day per mailbox once warm.
  5. Test before launch: two internal contacts per draft, one with a first name and company, one with there and your company. Pass when both render with no raw braces and no gap, the date reads Thursday 12 November, 10:00 UK time (11:00 CET), and the link opens the registration page with its utm_content.
  6. Start the thread at least 8 days before the session so the last step lands before it.
  7. After a week, keep the template with the most registrations per 100 people reached (utm_content), and retire the weakest.

No postal address: owner decision 2026-10-06, cold email ends with the unsubscribe line only (context/from-workshop-project/03-calendar-infra/cold-email.md).
