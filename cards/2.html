Enterprise ICP and Segments: EU and UK, English First

Adi, 2026-11-05: "Let's focus on EU and UK, but ideally English-speaking contacts for the upcoming webinar." The webinar is in English, weekly on Thursdays at 10:00 UK time, first on 12 November 2026. So this series invites EU and UK work addresses only, English-speaking countries first. The earlier build did the opposite (it held these rows back and targeted the rest); that build is archived outside the repo.

Who We Invite

The buyer of Tantra Enterprise ($9,999) wants the funnel built with them, with coaching, and has $10,000 or more to invest in the next 12 months (offer.fit in deliverables/_shared/funnel-config.json): the founder, owner or revenue lead of a B2B company where a person on a call closes the deal (context/from-tantra-content/02-target-audience.md). B2B software, consultancies and agencies, financial services, staffing and executive search, and course or coaching businesses with a team.

Not the buyer: schools, universities, public bodies, consumer businesses, and anyone without $10,000 to put behind a build.

How "English-Speaking" Is Defined

Three tiers by country ending. Tier 1 goes first, tier 3 only once tiers 1 and 2 are used up.

Tier Countries (domain endings) Why Usable
1: English is the native or official business language United Kingdom (.uk, .london, .scot, .wales, .cymru), Ireland (.ie), Malta (.mt) English is an official language in all three 127,218
2: very high business English Netherlands (.nl), Sweden (.se), Denmark (.dk), Finland (.fi), Norway (.no), Iceland (.is) Business is routinely done in English with outside partners; English is taught from primary school 79,393
3: the rest of the EU, used to reach volume Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Estonia, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, and .eu English is common in international firms but is not the default working language 187,431

The three non-EU calls:

  • Norway and Iceland: tier 2. Both are in the EEA, so the same GDPR rules apply as in the EU, and their business English is on a par with Sweden and Denmark.
  • Switzerland: tier 3. Business runs in German, French and Italian; English is strong in international firms but not the default. Its data law (the Swiss FADP) is its own, not GDPR, and the B2B rules below are the floor we hold it to.
  • Liechtenstein: tier 3 (EEA, German-speaking).

Left out: Jersey, Guernsey and the Isle of Man (.je, .gg, .im: own data laws, and .gg is mostly gaming companies), Gibraltar (.gi), and every country outside Europe.

Rows with a past Yes, Maybe or registered no-show (the warm list, 1,835 rows) go first whatever their tier: they already signed up for an English webinar like this one.

Per-Domain Caps and the Re-Invite Gap

  • At most 3 invites to one recipient domain a week and 30 over the plan. One UK company holds thousands of addresses; hundreds of cold invites into one company get our senders blocked by its filter. Each recipient domain is tied to one sender pool, so one company only hears from one Workspace.
  • Every row was already invited by other senders between 2026-07-22 and 2026-10-19. No row goes into a week whose send window opens less than 60 days after its last invite.

GDPR and PECR Note: B2B Cold Invites to EU and UK Work Addresses

What this list and its invites hold to. This is the working rule for this funnel, not legal advice.

  1. Legitimate interest, work addresses only. We invite people at their company address about a B2B service that fits their role. The list holds no personal mailboxes (Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo and similar), no role inboxes and nobody who declined before.
  2. An unsubscribe in every message. Every calendar invite description and every follow-up carries a one-line way to opt out (item 12 copy). Anyone who opts out or declines is never invited again: add them to the suppression list before the next week's import.
  3. No personal addresses, ever. If a vendor or a reply turns up a personal address, it stays off the cold list.

Adi's own check before the first send: lists.md, Outreach Rules by Country (d1_gdpr). That rule, not this note, is what releases a country.

The Honest Limits

  • 394,042 rows is under 4 weeks at full volume, and full volume only starts with the 18 February 2027 webinar (SLICING_PLAN.md). By the 25 February webinar the EU and UK rows in the master are used up. A title-filtered UK, Ireland, Nordics and Netherlands list is needed from early March 2027: VENDOR_PLAN.md.
  • Every row was invited once already, by senders that are not ours. Expect lower response than a fresh list.
  • Tier B is "B2B, segment unknown". Without titles we cannot tell a founder from an analyst.

SLICING_PLAN.md

The Weekly Slicing Plan: One Slice per Webinar, From 12 November 2026

One slice per Thursday webinar. S01 is the 12 November 2026 webinar, S02 the 19 November one, and so on. Each slice is sent in the 7 days before its webinar (Thursday to Wednesday), 7 send days a week.

A slice holds up to 100,000 rows, and never more than its senders can send that week. The 72 new senders (item 3, deliverables/03-outreach-infra/CAPACITY_PLAN.md) reach 200 a day with the 18 February 2027 webinar. Until then each slice is sized to the week's real capacity, so no EU or UK contact is spent on a week that cannot reach them. To cut every slice at a flat 100,000 instead, rerun with --caps flat.

Assumptions Behind the Dates

  • Day 0 = Monday 9 November 2026: every new Workspace has its $100 paid by then (team steps, item 3). If Day 0 moves, shift --caps by the same number of weeks and rerun.
  • P0 = the five older Tantra calendar senders (strategywebinars, visionarywebinars, seminarvision, nextgenwebinars, signonline) at 50 a day x 7 days = 1,750 a week. They are the only senders that can reach the 12 November to 3 December webinars, and only if they show Connected in Tantra and are past day 60 (team step 1, item 3). If they are not, rerun with --p0-caps 0 before Monday 9 November: S01 to S04 become empty and every row moves to later weeks.
  • P1 to P4 = Workspaces W1 to W4, 18 senders each on 3 domains. Every recipient domain is tied to one pool by a fixed hash.
  • No sends on 25 December 2026 or 1 January 2027 (6 send days in those two weeks).
