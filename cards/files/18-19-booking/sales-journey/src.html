3. Stage by stage

What happens at each stage, the software that runs it, and how it will be measured.

Actual = from the CRM once live (blank now)Target = Growth Idea benchmark, our plan, or FILLGreen on or above targetRed below target
Stage 01

List building

We cut the list of people to invite to each week's webinar.

  • The master calendar list (2,752,479 rows) filters down to 2,037,463 usable cold rows.
  • 1,000,000 rows go into 10 weekly slices of 100,000, after 10 ramp weeks led by the warm list.
  • No row is invited within 60 days of its last invite. 393,755 EU, UK and Swiss rows are held.
  • Each recipient domain is tied to one sender pool, so a company only hears from one Workspace.
Software

Master CSV · item 2 slicing script · Tantra (contact import)

Stage 02

Invites

Cold calendar invites put the webinar straight into prospects' calendars.

  • Tantra sends the invites from 440 senders on 80 domains, 8 to 12 days before the session.
  • Full volume, 100,000 a week, starts the week of 8 Feb 2027 if the Workspaces are paid by 9 Nov 2026.
  • Three cold email templates and LinkedIn messages run alongside.
  • A decline gets no follow-up. STOP in any channel sets Do Not Contact.

Stage 03

Registration

Registrants get a personal join link and a reminder run that gets them to the room.

  • The page form goes through the register Worker into {{webinar.platform}}, which emails the personal join link.
  • Yes and Maybe replies to the calendar invite are registered the same way.
  • Email and WhatsApp reminders run from 7 days out to 10 minutes after the start, plus up to two Vodex AI calls.
  • CANT moves a registrant to the replay track; CAL sends the calendar link.
Software

Registration page · Cloudflare Worker · {{webinar.platform}} · Tantra (email, WhatsApp) · Vodex · CRM sheet

Stage 04

Webinar

The weekly live webinar is where prospects decide whether to talk to us.

  • {{webinar.duration_minutes}} minutes on {{webinar.platform}}: {{webinar.presentation_minutes}} of teaching, {{webinar.qa_minutes}} of Q&A.
  • {{brand.host_name}} presents. A second presenter, camera off, runs chat, polls and the offer boxes at 11, 26 and 41 minutes.
  • Attendance splits people into stayed to offer ({{webinar.retarget_cutoff_minute}}+ minutes), early leaver and no-show.
  • Each segment gets its own 14-day follow-up track (item 22).

Stage 05

Booking

Attendees book the {{booking.event_title}}. The booking questions are the application.

  • One Cal.com round-robin link spreads bookings evenly between {{closer.0}} and {{closer.1}}.
  • The questions qualify the lead: revenue band, $10,000 to invest in 12 months, main channel, bottleneck and who signs off.
  • The closer confirms by phone or WhatsApp voice note inside 5 minutes.
  • Offer clicks with no booking get a call inside 5 minutes; cancellations get Seq. E.

Stage 06

Call 1

A {{booking.duration_minutes}}-minute call to find the stage that loses the most revenue and check fit.

  • Reminders 24 hours, 3 hours and 15 minutes before; a 10-minute research task 2 hours before.
  • The closer runs the discovery call script and logs the outcome within the hour.
  • Fit: wants it built with us and has $10,000 or more to invest in the next 12 months.
  • A missed call is not rescheduled while the rebooking switch is off.

Stage 07

Written proposal + due diligence

Between calls, the prospect sees what working with us would look like.

  • A written proposal within one business day: their situation, the leak, the build, the 3-month plan and the {{offer.price}} investment.
  • The due diligence page: review the proposal, watch the deep-dive and the replay, read the case studies and testimonials.
  • The proposal and Seq. D carry the closer's own Proposal Review link, with reminders to book it on days 1, 3 and 7. No Call 2 and no decision by day 8 moves the deal to Follow-Up.
Software

Proposal generator (item 24) · due diligence page (item 20) · Tantra · CRM sheet

Stage 08

Call 2

The {{booking.call2.event_title}}, where the decision is made.

  • {{booking.call2.duration_minutes}} minutes with the closer who ran Call 1, booked from the proposal email on that closer's own Cal.com link.
  • Reminders 24 hours, 3 hours and 15 minutes before; a prep task 2 hours before.
  • The closer walks through the proposal, answers objections from the FAQ and asks for the decision. A yes gets the agreement and payment link on the call.
  • No decision 7 days after Call 2 moves the deal to Follow-Up. A missed Call 2 follows the same rebooking switch as Call 1.
