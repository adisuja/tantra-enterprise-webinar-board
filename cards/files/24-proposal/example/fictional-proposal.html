How we build at Tantra

“Every stage gets a number, so you always know where the next sale is lost.”

In Phase 1, we take your numbers today, agree your buyer and your offer, and choose the conversion mechanism that fits it. Your platform account, tracking sheet and pipeline are set up before anything goes live.

Phase 2 is the build: traffic tracked to revenue, landing and registration pages, nurture across email, WhatsApp and AI voice, the conversion mechanism and the sales scripts.

In Phase 3, we run it live with your team beside us, review the numbers every week and fix Multi-channel nurture first.

Then comes Phase 4: your team runs the engine and we coach. The 52-week follow-up keeps turning your list into revenue after month 3.

We have worked with 400+ brands in 47+ industries across 21+ countries over 10+ years. This one we build with you.
