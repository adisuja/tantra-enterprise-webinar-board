{{offer.name}} ({{offer.price}}) is 3 months of 1:1 coaching and implementation, a done-with-you build of the client's funnel, 1 year of the Tantra platform, and the course, community and group coaching calls. The client builds with us in weeks 1 to 6, launches their first webinars in weeks 7 to 10 with a numbers review after each, and from week 11 runs it themselves with coaching, on the platform to month 12. The renewal at month 12 is the platform, not the coaching. Steps marked DRAFT come from the draft plan and are not confirmed; nothing here promises a result.
