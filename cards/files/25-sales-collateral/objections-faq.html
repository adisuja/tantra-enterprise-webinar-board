Objections and FAQ for Tantra Enterprise Calls

Internal, for Conor and Scott, on both calls. Not for clients. Every answer uses approved proof only. Lines marked Adi to confirm wait on Adi before you say them.

Five Rules on Every Call

  • Only two clients may be named: Growth Idea and FintechCircle, with their measured numbers below. Any other past client is "a client", never a name.
  • No guarantee of results, no invented deadline, no discount, no bonus. None exist.
  • No pipeline or revenue figure for any client. Only calls, MQLs and clients won.
  • Price is $9,999. Platform access is 1 year.
  • Not a fit: say so on the call, and no proposal. A fit: the decision is made on Call 2, the Proposal Review.

"$9,999 is a lot."

Really asking: will this pay back?

"It is a real investment, so let's test it against your numbers, not mine. What is one new client worth to you over a year? And how many extra sales calls a month would it take to win one?"

Then: "For that, my team builds all six stages with yours over 3 months, you get a year on the platform, and your people learn to run it. You are not renting it from us."

Never: an ROI promise, "it pays for itself", or a discount.

"Now is not the right time."

Really asking: can this wait without costing me?

"Fair. What changes between now and then? If the answer is 'nothing, we will just be busier', the pipeline problem is still there next quarter."

If the timing is genuinely wrong (budget not there, no one to own it), agree, note why, and let the follow-up sequence do its job. No pressure close.

Never: a fake deadline or "price goes up next week".

"We tried agencies. It did not work."

Really asking: why would you be different?

"What went wrong? Usually it is one of two things: they ran it in a black box, or they left and took the know-how with them."

"Enterprise is done with you. Your team is in the build, the numbers sit in a sheet you can see every week, and by week 13 your people run it with us coaching. That is how both case studies ran: one tracker, every webinar logged."

Never: criticise a named agency.

"We already have a marketing team."

Really asking: is this paying for what I already have?

"Good, that makes it work better. We do not replace them. We build alongside them, bring the six-stage system and the platform, and coach them until they run it alone."

"Who on your team would own it day to day? Let's put them on the proposal by name."

"Will it work in my industry?"

Really asking: has anyone like me done this?

"Adi has built revenue engines for 400+ brands across 47+ industries and 21+ countries. The two I can show you in detail are a UK business-coaching group and a London fintech network."

"I will not promise your numbers. What I can do is map your buyer and your channels on this call. If we do not think it fits your market, I will tell you now."

Never: claim a past client in their industry unless it is one of the two named cases.

"What happens after year 1? Are we locked in?"

Really asking: will I be held hostage by the platform?

"Year 1 on the platform is included. After that you choose whether to continue. Today's self-serve plans are Pro at $99 a month and Scale at $199 a month."

Adi to confirm which plan and price Enterprise clients move to after year 1, and that clients can export their contacts, pages and sequences if they leave. Do not promise either until confirmed.

"Can you guarantee results?"

Really asking: what if it fails?

"No, and be wary of anyone who does. What I can show you is what two clients recorded in their own trackers, and the plan: we measure every week against the targets you set, and fix the leakiest stage first."

Never: a money-back, a results guarantee or "you will get X calls".

"Why not take Pro at $99 a month and do it ourselves?"

Really asking: am I overbuying?

"You might be right. Pro is for teams who want to learn the system and build it themselves. Enterprise is for when you want it built with you, by people who have built it before, with 3 months of 1:1 coaching. Which one sounds like you?"

If Pro fits better, say so. A wrong-fit Enterprise client hurts everyone.

"How much of my time does this take?"

Really asking: can I hand it off?

"You decide the offer, the buyer and the price, and you present the conversion event. Day to day, one owner on your side handles access, approvals and numbers."

Adi to confirm the hours a week to quote and the coaching call cadence.

"Is ad spend included? What other costs are there?"

Really asking: what is the real total?

"The $9,999 covers the build, the coaching, 1 year of platform and the course and community. If you run paid ads, you pay the ad platforms directly. Any outside tools or data you choose to buy are yours too."

Adi to confirm this not-included list before the first proposal.

"Can we pay in instalments?"

Really asking: can I spread the cash?

"Payment terms are set in the written proposal you get within one business day." Adi to confirm the standard terms; until then, promise nothing on the call.

"We do not do webinars."

Really asking: is this only one tactic?

"The conversion mechanism is chosen to fit your offer: a live event, a video sales funnel or a multi-day workshop. Our two case studies used webinars because they fit those offers. We pick yours together in weeks 1 to 2."

"Can I see more case studies, or talk to a client?"

Really asking: is this real?

"I can walk you through two in full detail, with the numbers from their own trackers. We only name clients who have cleared it."

Adi to confirm whether a reference call is ever offered. Never offer one until confirmed.
