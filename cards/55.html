AI calling in the Tantra CRM: spec for the 24-hour prototype (card 55)

Written 10 November 2026. Nothing is built yet. This is the spec for Adi to confirm.

In one line

An "AI call" step in the CRM sequence builder, next to SMS, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and email. First two jobs: a confirmation call when someone registers for the webinar, and a batch reminder call just before it starts. Vodex places the calls now (prepaid credits); Vapi, then Retell, sit behind the same adapter for the long term.

What I found in the code

  • The CRM Worker is the only place this fits in 24 hours. It already has the sequence engine, the cron that runs every minute, webinar intake (the ew-registered tag), consent records, a calls table and a timeline that plays call recordings. Adding a channel is one file on the backend and two on the frontend.
  • The main Tantra app cannot take it yet. Its steps are whole-day delays only, with no telephony and no usage ledger. It gets the feature when the CRM merges in.
  • One real design gap. The engine sends and moves on. A call finishes minutes later, so the step needs a "waiting for the call result" state that the provider's webhook closes.
  • A Vodex client already exists in the workshop app (verify key, save agent, trigger calls). I reuse its request shapes.
  • Vodex account today: credit for about 666 minutes, one project ready ("Gc-Webinar Calls", agent Lilly), one Indian caller ID, voicemail detection on. Campaigns were triggered by API in 2025, so a key was issued before. The key is not on this Mac or in the mailbox I can search.

Product framing (Product-Manager-Skills: epic hypothesis, PoL probe, story split)

Epic hypothesis. If I add an AI call step to CRM sequences for webinar registrants, then more of them show up live, because a call gets answered when an email does not. Measured on our own webinar: show-up rate of called registrants against not-called registrants in the same week, plus answer rate and cost per answered call.

PoL probe (the cheapest test that tells the truth). One API-triggered Vodex call to Adi's own Indian number, with the result landing on a deal's timeline. If that fails (key, caller ID, webhook), nothing else matters, so it goes first.

Step outcomes the sequence can branch on: answered, voicemail, no answer, busy, failed, opted out.

Three calls I made that you should check

  1. Vapi first, Retell second. Vapi gives the most control and uses our own Twilio numbers. Retell is the fallback and the voice-quality benchmark.
  2. The reminder starts 15 minutes before, not 5. 98 registrants cannot all be dialled in the same minute: Vodex and the engine both cap how many go out at once. Starting at T-15 means everyone has been called by T-5.
  3. A consent line on the registration form. US law needs written consent for an AI voice call. Wording: "I agree to receive a reminder call, which may use an AI voice, at the number I gave." Without it, the call step skips that person.
