README.md

27 Retargeting: Early Leavers and No-Shows, Six Weeks

Registrants who left before minute 25 or never showed get six weeks of email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn via Tantra and Meta and LinkedIn ads, then drop out. The script turns each WebinarJam export into hashed ad audiences. It reads segments only and never posts CRM events: item 17's import_attendance is the only producer of attendance events.

Early leavers (before minute 25) have now seen the initial pitch at minute 20 (deck slide 23: what Tantra Enterprise is, who it is for, $9,999, and the Evaluation Call, proposal and Proposal Review path; QR at minute 22), re-timed 2026-11-05.

Notes for the Lead

  • Early leaver is measured by minutes in the room (contract), not the minute someone left; a late joiner who stayed 10 minutes counts as an early leaver.
  • LinkedIn upload format: the LinkedIn files carry one column, email, SHA-256 of the lowercase address. That is our reading of LinkedIn's contact-list spec, not yet tested: on the first upload, check Campaign Manager accepts it and reports a match rate; if not, switch to its current template.
  • Exclusions file: must include booked, replied, opted out, re-registered, won and lost; the script stops if it has no email column or no rows.
  • Ads use "one client" with no name; ad creatives need Adi's Kie.ai avatar (item 5 to 10 design or a new render).

RETARGETING_PLAN.md

27 Retargeting Plan: Six Weeks, Then Dropped

Who: registrants who left before minute 25 or never showed. What: six weeks of email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn via Tantra, and Meta and LinkedIn ad audiences. Then they drop out, unless they book, reply or opt out first. Copy: COPY.md. Imports: email.csv, whatsapp.csv, linkedin.csv, ads.csv. Code: retarget_segments.py.

Segment Rules (CRM_CONTRACT.md)

Segment Rule Retargeted?
early_leaver Attended and minutes_in_room under 25 (webinar.retarget_cutoff_minute) Yes, 6 weeks
no_show Registered and marked not attended by WebinarJam Yes, 6 weeks
stayed_to_offer Attended and minutes_in_room 25 or more No (item 22 track A, then the newsletter)
  • retarget_until = webinar date + 42 days. Day 42 is the drop day: active on days 0 to 41, out on day 42.
  • A contact's latest webinar decides. Someone who stayed to the offer last month and missed this week is a no-show now.
  • Attendance rule (CRM_CONTRACT, same as items 17 and 23): if WebinarJam marks the person as attended, they attended. minutes_in_room is a whole number floored at 1: a blank or 0 time becomes 1, so an attendee is never a no_show and never has empty minutes. Only people WebinarJam marks as not attended are no_show.
  • Minutes come from the export's time-in-room column (a two-part time like 1:05 is read as H:MM unless webinarjam_columns.json sets colon_format to m:ss). Several rows for one email and date (rejoins) are merged: attended if any row attended, minutes summed.
  • The CRM Webinar tab is the record. Item 17's import_attendance is the only producer of webinar.attended and webinar.no_show; this script posts nothing and only builds audiences with the same rule.
  • Edge case to know: someone who joined at minute 20 and stayed 10 minutes counts as an early leaver, because the contract measures time in the room, not the minute they left.

What happens When Effect
Six weeks pass Day 42 after the webinar (the script drops on as-of >= retarget_until) Out of every message and audience. Into the Beehiiv newsletter only if newsletter_opt_in
Books a call (booking.created) Any time Out at once; booking sequences take over; added to the exclusion audience
Replies (contact.replied) Any time Out at once; a person answers; added to the exclusion audience

Won or lost (deal.won, deal.lost) Any time Out at once; added to the exclusion audience

Ad Audiences: Weekly Refresh

Every week, the day after the webinar, once the WebinarJam export is in:

  1. Export the session's attendance from WebinarJam (CSV) into ~/tantra-private/27-retargeting/exports/ (never the repo).
  2. Export the exclusions from the CRM: everyone with booking.created, contact.replied, contact.opted_out, a later webinar.registered (re-registered), deal.won or deal.lost. Columns email, reason (reason: booked, replied, opted_out, reregistered, won, lost). The script stops if the file has no email column or no rows, so a broken export can never retarget people who opted out.
  3. Run: python3 deliverables/27-retargeting/retarget_segments.py \ --exports ~/tantra-private/27-retargeting/exports/*.csv \ --exclude ~/tantra-private/27-retargeting/crm_exclusions.csv \ --as-of <today, YYYY-MM-DD> --out ~/tantra-private/27-retargeting Give it every export from the last six weeks, so audiences roll: new people in, people on day 42 or later out. Rejoins (the same email twice for one date) are merged and their minutes summed.
  4. In Meta Ads Manager and LinkedIn Campaign Manager, replace each audience's list with the new file of the same name (replace, not add, so dropped and excluded people leave).
Audience (file) Meta LinkedIn Ads
early_leaver_replay meta_early_leaver_replay.csv linkedin_early_leaver_replay.csv *-replay-1
no_show_replay meta_no_show_replay.csv linkedin_no_show_replay.csv *-replay-1
early_leaver_call meta_early_leaver_call.csv linkedin_early_leaver_call.csv -call-1, -call-2
no_show_call meta_no_show_call.csv linkedin_no_show_call.csv -call-1, -call-2
exclude (every ad set) meta_exclude.csv linkedin_exclude.csv excluded from all

Hashing. Meta: SHA-256 of normalised values, columns email (trimmed, lowercase), phone (digits with country code, no plus), fn and ln (lowercase, no punctuation or spaces), country (2-letter ISO, lowercase). LinkedIn: one column email, SHA-256 of the trimmed, lowercase address. LinkedIn's single hashed email column is our reading of its contact-list spec: on the first upload, check Campaign Manager accepts it and shows a match rate; if it rejects the file, use its current template. Meta needs about 100 matched people and LinkedIn 300 matched members before an audience serves: summary.json warns when a file is smaller. Early on, run one ad set on the replay and call audiences together until each is big enough.

WebinarJam Export: Column Assumption

The script has not seen a real WebinarJam export yet. It expects these columns (any case): Email, First Name, Last Name, Phone, Country, Event Date, Attended Live, Time in Live Room, Entered Live At, Left Live At. Each field also accepts the alternatives listed in webinarjam_columns.json. On the first real export, put its exact header names first in that file; summary.json shows which column matched each field. Only Email is required; with no date column, pass --webinar-date.
