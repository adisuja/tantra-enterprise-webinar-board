On screen: You Have Already Seen the Funnel. Now See the Program.

You're watching this because you sat through the live session, or the replay, and something clicked.

Here's the problem. Knowing how the funnel works is not the same as having one running in your business. I've watched a lot of founders take good notes, open a blank doc, and stall at step one.

So in the next ten minutes I'm going to show you exactly what Tantra Enterprise is, what you get, how it runs, what it costs, and how to decide if it's right for you.

No fluff. Let's go.

On screen: We Build It With You.

Tantra Enterprise is our highest level of help.

In one sentence: my team builds your funnel with you, coaches your team while we do it, and gives you the platform to run it on.

That last part matters. You don't get a course and a pat on the back. You get people who have built this before, working on your funnel, inside your business.

It's $9,999.

I'll come back to the price. First, let me show you who this is for, because it is not for everyone.

Tantra Enterprise is a fit if two things are true.

Two: you have $10,000 or more to invest in growth in the next 12 months.

That second one is not me being picky. A build like this takes real hours from my team and real attention from yours. If the budget isn't there yet, the smarter move is to run what you learned in the live session yourself, then come back.

And if you're thinking "I'd rather build it alone", that's fine too. The live session gave you the map. Enterprise is for founders who want the map and the crew.

4. What You Get (2:30 to 4:50)

On screen: Four Things You Get.

Here's exactly what's included. Four things.

Number one: 3 months of 1:1 coaching and implementation with your team.

This is the core. For three months you work directly with us. Not a group call where you wait your turn. One to one, on your business, your offer and your numbers.

And it's implementation, not just advice. When we agree something needs to change, it gets changed. Your team learns by doing it with us, so when the three months end, they can run it.

Number two: a done-with-you build of your funnel.

Done-with-you means both teams build it. We bring the structure and the experience. You bring your offer, your market and your voice.

That's the funnel you saw in the live session: the traffic that brings people in, the page they register on, the reminders that get them there, the session that turns them into calls, and the pipeline that follows up.

Your written proposal sets the exact scope and timeline for your build, based on what we find on the evaluation call.

Number three: 1 year of access to the Tantra platform.

The funnel needs somewhere to run. Tantra is the platform my team runs these engines on. You get a full year of access, so the build doesn't stop working the day coaching ends.

One year. Not a lifetime. Not ten years. One year, and I'd rather be clear about that now than surprise you later.

Number four: the full course, the community and the group coaching calls.

Between your 1:1 sessions you're not on your own. You get the full course to go deeper on any part of the funnel, the community to ask questions, and the group coaching calls where you hear what's working for other founders right now.

So that's it. 1:1 coaching and implementation for three months. Your funnel, built with you. A year on the platform. And the course, community and group calls around all of it.

On screen: Call. Proposal. Proposal Review. Build.

Here's how you go from this page to a funnel running in your business. Four steps.

Step one: the evaluation call.

It's a 30-minute call with my team, booked on Cal.com. We look at your offer, your buyers and your numbers today. We find the stage where your funnel is losing the most. And we decide together if Enterprise is the right fit.

If it isn't, we'll tell you on the call. I'd rather lose a sale than sign a client we can't help.

One thing to know: this time is held for you, and my team prepares for it. If you need to move it, use the link in your confirmation email. A missed call is not rescheduled.

Step two: your written proposal.

If it's a fit, you get a written proposal within one business day of the call. Scope, timeline, and who on your team needs to be involved.

That's the document Step 1 on this page refers to. Read it properly. Share it with whoever signs off on growth spend.

Step three: the Proposal Review.

Then you book a Proposal Review call with the same person you spoke to on the first call. We go through the proposal line by line, answer every question, and you decide on that call.

Step four: the build.

You say yes, and we start. Three months of 1:1 coaching and implementation, the done-with-you build, and the platform from day one.

On screen: $9,999.

Tantra Enterprise is $9,999.

Here's how I'd think about it. You are not paying for information. You've already had the information: it was in the live session, and it's in the replay on this page.

You're paying for three things you can't get from a video. A team that has built this before, working on yours. Three months of someone holding the work to a standard. And a platform to run it on for a year.

Both. That's what done-with-you means. My team builds alongside yours and coaches them as we go. By the end of the three months, the people in your business know how the funnel works and how to run it, because they helped build it.

The coaching ends. The funnel doesn't. You still have the platform for the rest of your year, plus the course, the community and the group coaching calls. That's the point of the year: the engine keeps running while your team takes it over.

Then don't buy it. Seriously. If you'd rather run it yourself on the platform, there's Pro at $99 a month, with a free trial, and Scale at $199 a month. We'll tell you on the evaluation call if one of those is the better fit. You'll never get pushed into something that's wrong for your business.

How the config works

Every title, date, time, link and price comes from deliverables/_shared/funnel-config.json. A value starting FILL_ renders as an amber pill naming the token. Fill it once, run ./publish.sh, and every page updates. The title falls back to webinar.working_title.

  • Countdowns: once webinar.first_date, webinar.start_time_local and webinar.timezone are set, every D/H/M/S box and every "Live session starts in" line counts down to that session. Cadence weekly rolls a past date forward to the next week's session. Until then the boxes read 00 and the pills say what is missing. There is never an evergreen timer. Checked with a test config (2026-11-12 11:00 America/New_York): boxes counted down, text timer read "7 days and 20 hours".
  • Live form: when integrations.register_worker_url and integrations.turnstile_site_key are both filled, Turnstile renders in the popup and the form posts to the Worker (item 17). Otherwise the review stub: no external script, no request.
  • Replay: every replay slot (the /recording/ hero and the due diligence Step 2 "Webinar Replay" row) reads webinar.replay_url. WebinarJam records each session; the replay link will be filled later through the WebinarJam API (Adi 2026-11-04). That API step is not built yet. Until then the slot is a designed placeholder with the FILL_REPLAY_URL pill.
  • Videos: the VSL, the four /watch/ videos, the Program Deep-Dive and the case videos stay designed placeholders (play mark, title, "Video added once it is recorded", the FILL pill and the script file) until Adi records them; each fills from one config key.
