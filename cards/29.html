  2. Side profiles: one Kie.ai nano-banana-pro image per person, made from that person's own photos (passed by their Drive link), strict side view facing left, black background, violet rim light: src/side/. src/side/metrics.json stores each nose tip and head top, so every face lands on the same spot at the right edge.

  4. Check: node build.mjs --guides draws the photo zones; node mock.mjs drops each cover into LinkedIn sized cards.
