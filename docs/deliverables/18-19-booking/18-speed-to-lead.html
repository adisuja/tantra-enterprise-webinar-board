Speed to Lead: Every Booking Confirmed Inside 5 Minutes

A booked call is a promise the lead made while they were excited. Every hour you wait, that excitement cools and the no-show risk climbs. This is the process Conor and Scott run so every Enterprise Evaluation Call is confirmed by a human inside 5 minutes.

Offer on the call: Tantra Enterprise, $9,999. Call 1: 30 minutes on Google Meet, booked through the Cal.com round-robin link (item 19). Promise: a written proposal within one business day, carrying the closer's own Proposal Review link. Call 2: the 30-minute Proposal Review with the same closer, where the decision is made (section 7; approved by Adi 2026-11-05). WhatsApp replaces SMS everywhere.

The setter is whoever the Lists tab names in setter_name. Empty (the default) means the assigned closer is also the setter. Decision for Adi, section 9.

Minute Who Action

Rules: - Night bookings. A booking between 20:00 and 08:00 in the lead's timezone gets the call or voice note at 08:00 their time, not at night. The runner holds the WhatsApp the same way. - Cover. If the assigned closer is on a call, the other closer does minutes 1 to 5 and writes "covered by" in Pipeline notes. The Summary tab's "Rep tasks open" shows anything left. - Never cancel or move a booking for the lead on this call. Send them the reschedule link from their confirmation email.

3. Call-Confirmation Script

Phone (about 60 seconds)

Hi {first name}, it's {Conor or Scott} from Tantra. You just booked your Enterprise Evaluation Call for {day} at {time}. Have I caught you at an OK moment?

Great. I'm the one you'll speak with, so two quick things to make the 30 minutes worth it.

One: join from a laptop with your camera on, and have your offer, your pricing and your rough monthly numbers to hand.

Two: you said {sign-off answer}. If someone else signs off on growth investment, bring them. We decide on the call whether this is a fit, and that's much easier with them there.

Last thing: the time is held only for you and I prepare before we speak. If anything changes, move it at least 24 hours ahead with the link in your confirmation email.

Does {time} on {day} still work?

Yes: "Perfect. I'll send a WhatsApp reminder before we start. Speak then." Mark Confirmed.

The time doesn't work: "No problem. Use the reschedule link in your confirmation email and pick a slot that does." Do not book for them.

They answered "no" to the $10,000 question: "You mentioned the $10,000 isn't there yet. Is that timing, or budget?" Timing: keep the call. Budget: "This call is about Tantra Enterprise, which is $9,999. Do you still want the slot?" Either answer is fine; note it in Pipeline notes.

WhatsApp voice note (about 30 seconds, when the call goes unanswered)

Hi {first name}, {Conor or Scott} from Tantra. You're booked for your Enterprise Evaluation Call on {day} at {time}, and I'm the one you'll speak with. Have your offer, pricing and rough monthly numbers ready, and bring whoever signs off on growth investment. Just reply YES to this message so I know the time still works.

4. Pre-Call Research Checklist (10 Minutes, Due 2 Hours Before)

The prep task lands 2 hours before the call. Tick every line, then write three lines in Pipeline notes.

  • [ ] Read the five booking answers: revenue band, $10,000+ in 12 months, main channel, biggest bottleneck, who signs off.
  • [ ] Webinar tab: did they attend, how many minutes, did they stay past minute 25, what did they answer in the polls, did they click the offer?
  • [ ] Their website: what they sell, at what price, to whom, and how a buyer gets in today (book a call, checkout, demo, application).
  • [ ] Their funnel: is there a webinar, a video sales letter, a lead magnet or a booking page? Note the step that looks weakest.
  • [ ] Their traffic: check the main channel they named against what you can see (founder's LinkedIn activity, ads, content).
  • [ ] Rough math: monthly revenue band divided by price point gives the customers a month they win now.
  • [ ] Sign-off: who signs, and is that person coming? If not, plan to ask for a second call with them.
  • [ ] Pick the one client example you may need, from PROOF_PACK only (Growth Idea or FintechCircle measured numbers).
  • [ ] Pipeline notes, three lines: (1) the stage you think loses the most revenue, (2) the mechanism that likely fits (live event, video sales funnel or multi-day workshop), (3) the first question you will ask.

7. Call 2: The Proposal Review

Call 2 is a 30-minute call on Google Meet, booked by the prospect from the proposal email, always with the closer who ran Call 1. Each closer has their own Cal.com event type, Tantra Enterprise Proposal Review, slug proposal-review (item 19, section 8). The deal stays in 5 Proposal Sent the whole time; call2_date and call2_status on the Pipeline row show where Call 2 is.

If a Call 2 is booked on the other closer's link, the deal keeps its Call 1 closer and the alert says so. Run the call yourself or hand it over in Pipeline notes.

8. After Closed Won

The sales pipeline ends at payment. The handover follows the payment-to-onboarding order in the client-journey reference: payment and "marked won" trigger onboarding, then a welcome message with the onboarding booking link, then the pre-onboarding checklist, then the onboarding call.

  • W1 (instant): welcome WhatsApp and email with onboarding_link (Lists tab; FILL until Adi sets it).
  • W2 (instant): closer task to hand over notes, proposal and the bottleneck the client named.
  • W3 (+14 days): closer task to ask for a written testimonial, quoted word for word, with permission to publish.

9. Decisions for Adi

  1. No-show rebooking (conflict). The playbook's Sequence C rebooks no-shows. Your corrected call description says: "If the call is missed without notice, we won't be able to reschedule it or arrange another conversation in the future." Built: Sequence C in full behind the Lists tab setting seq_c_rebook, default OFF so the live promise holds (C1 "I'm on the call now" still goes; C2 to C4 rebook messages are skipped; the contact never gets a re-engage WhatsApp). Turn it ON to run the playbook. Pick one so the page and the process say the same thing.
  2. Who is the setter? Default: the assigned closer confirms their own bookings. Name a separate setter in setter_name and setter_email if you want one.
  3. Proposal timing. The playbook says send it within 1 hour of the call; your description promises one business day. Built to the description, with the task telling closers to aim for the same day.
  4. How messages send. Default queue: the closer sends each due WhatsApp and email from Tantra (the CRM emails them the exact text). tantra mode sends through the Tantra API as single-step campaigns; it is untested on the live account because Tantra has no "add one person to a running campaign" call. Keep queue until one test send passes.
  5. Not ready end date. The playbook has no exit for Seq. F. Built: a deal still in Showed 14 days after the 30-day check-in moves to Nurture. Confirm or change.
  6. Call 2 booking window. How many days after the proposal can Call 2 be booked (booking.call2.book_within_days, FILL; suggested 3 business days)? It sets Limit future bookings on the two Proposal Review event types. The copy names no number of days, so nothing changes in the messages.
  7. Missed Call 2. Built to follow the same seq_c_rebook switch as a missed Call 1 (OFF: no rebooking, Nurture on day 7). Say if a missed Call 2, which comes after a written proposal, should always get the rebook messages instead.
