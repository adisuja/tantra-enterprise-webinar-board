Call 2 Script: Tantra Enterprise Proposal Review (30 Minutes)

For Conor and Scott. The prospect has read your written proposal and booked this call from the proposal email, on your own Proposal Review link. You ran their Call 1, so you run this one. This is the call where the decision is made: by minute 30 you have a yes, a no, or the one thing still in the way, written down.

Approved by Adi on 2026-11-05 (BLOCKERS A12). Proof comes from PROOF_PACK.md only. Objection answers come from the FAQ: objections-faq.html (PDF: deliverables/25-sales-collateral/objections-faq-a4.pdf).

Minutes Part What you leave with
Before Prep (task 2 hours before) The proposal and Call 1 notes re-read; agreement and payment link ready
0 to 3 Open Who is on the call, and the agreement to decide today
3 to 15 Walk through the proposal Each section confirmed or corrected
15 to 24 Questions and objections Every question answered, or written down with a date
24 to 30 Decide Yes (agreement and payment link sent), No (reason), or the one thing missing

Before the Call (10 Minutes, Due 2 Hours Before)

  • [ ] Re-read the proposal you sent (proposal_url on the Pipeline row) and your three lines of Call 1 notes.
  • [ ] Check the Pipeline row: did they open a reply, ask anything, or forward it? Anything in notes since Call 1?
  • [ ] Have the agreement and the payment link for $9,999 ready to send while you are still on the call. Tool and payment terms: FILL until Adi sets them (BLOCKERS A4). Until then, promise no terms on the call.
  • [ ] Guess the two questions they are most likely to ask, and find each in the FAQ.
  • [ ] Sign-off: is the person who signs on the invite? If not, plan the ask in "Decide".

If they say yes before the call (a reply to the proposal email), do not make them wait for it: send the agreement and payment link, and keep the call only if they want it.

0 to 3: Open

Thanks for making the time, {first name}. You've had the proposal for a day or two, so here's how I'd like to use the 30 minutes. I'll walk you through it quickly, section by section, and you stop me wherever something isn't how you see it. Then I'll answer every question you and {sign-off person} have. And before we hang up we'll decide together: go ahead, don't go ahead, or name the one thing still in the way. Fair?

If the person who signs off is not there: "Is {name} joining? This is the call where we decide, so it's much easier with them here." If they cannot join, run the call and use the "someone else signs" row in Decide.

3 to 15: Walk Through the Proposal

Share your screen with the proposal open. One question per section, then move on. Do not read it to them.

Proposal section Say Ask
Scenario overview "This is what you told me on our first call, in your words." "Is that still where you are?"
Funnel strategy "This is the stage we fix first: {leak stage}." "Does that still look like the biggest leak to you?"
3-month plan "Here's what happens in which weeks, and when you see the first live run." "Does that timeline fit what's happening in your business?"
Your role "This is who does what on each side." "Who on your team owns it day to day?"
Investment "$9,999: three months of 1:1 coaching and implementation, the done-with-you build, a year on the platform, and the course, community and group coaching calls." "Does the scope match what you need for that?"

Anything they correct: write it in Pipeline notes and say "I'll change that in the agreement." Never argue a correction.

15 to 24: Questions and Objections

Answer only the objection they raise, with the FAQ answer, then ask one question back. Never say a line the FAQ marks "Adi to confirm" until Adi has confirmed it.

They say FAQ card Ask back
"It's a lot of money." "$9,999 is a lot." "If it paid back inside the three months, would it still be too much?"
"Not the right time." "Now is not the right time." "What changes between now and then?"
"We tried agencies." "We tried agencies. It did not work." "What went wrong last time?"
"We have a marketing team." "We already have a marketing team." "Who on your team would own it day to day?"
"Will it work for us?" "Will it work in my industry?" "Which part of the plan worries you most?"
"Are we locked in after year 1?" "What happens after year 1? Are we locked in?" Adi to confirm the answer first
"Can you guarantee it?" "Can you guarantee results?" "What would you need to see in the first 6 weeks?"
"Why not Pro at $99?" "Why not take Pro at $99 a month and do it ourselves?" "Which one sounds like you?"
"How much of my time?" "How much of my time does this take?" Adi to confirm the hours first
"What else will it cost?" "Is ad spend included? What other costs are there?" "Do you plan to run paid ads in the first 3 months?"
"Can we pay in instalments?" "Can we pay in instalments?" Promise nothing until Adi sets the terms
"We don't do webinars." "We do not do webinars." "Which of the three fits how your buyers decide?"
"Can I talk to a client?" "Can I see more case studies, or talk to a client?" Never offer a reference call until Adi confirms one

Proof, only if it helps, one line, measured numbers only: - Growth Idea: 11 webinars, May 2025 to Feb 2026, 6,517 registered, 1,384 came live, 80 sales calls booked, 4 clients won. - FintechCircle: outreach grew from 10,000 reached to 20,046 and then 30,000 calendar invites across three episodes (March to April 2026), which booked 17 calls.

24 to 30: Decide

Fit statement, then one question:

So the plan is: we fix {leak stage} first, build {mechanism} with {owner} over three months, and your team runs it from month 4. Is there anything stopping you from going ahead today?

Answer Say Do now Log in the CRM
Yes "Great. I'm sending the agreement and the payment link now, while we're on the call." Send both while you are still on the call. Confirm they arrived Notes, then Tantra CRM, Call 2 Showed. Closed Won when the payment lands
Yes, but someone else signs "Let's get {them} 15 minutes with me this week so they hear it first hand." Book it from your Proposal Review link while you are on the call Notes, Call 2 Showed; the new booking keeps the deal in Proposal Sent
Need to think "Of course. What exactly do you want to think through?" Answer that one thing. If it can't be answered now: "When will you know?" Agree a date and write it in next_follow_up Notes with the one thing, Call 2 Showed. Seq. RS checks in on day 2; Follow-Up from day 7
Not now "Understood. What would need to change for this to be the right time?" Nothing to send Notes, then Closed Lost with reason Timing (goes to Nurture)
No "Thanks for telling me straight. Can I ask what made the difference?" Nothing to send Notes, then Closed Lost with the reason (Price, Competitor, Not a fit)

Never invent a deadline, discount, bonus or guarantee to get the yes. None exist.

After the Call

Within one hour: notes, then the menu action on the Pipeline row: Call 2 Showed or Call 2 No-Show (the outcome task at start + 45 minutes reminds you). Then Closed Won when the payment lands, or Closed Lost with a reason. A Call 2 left at Booked keeps reminding you and blocks the follow-up from starting.

If they did not join: Cal.com, Bookings, Past, the booking, mark the attendee as no-show (or Tantra CRM, Call 2 No-Show). The "I'm on the call now" WhatsApp goes straight away. While seq_c_rebook is OFF, no rebooking messages go, the same rule as a missed Call 1, and the deal moves to Nurture on day 7.
