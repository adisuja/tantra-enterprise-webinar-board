Sequences A to F, Nurture, Won and Rep Tasks

Generated by deliverables/23-crm/build.py from sequences.json. Edit the JSON, then run the build: the CRM runner sends exactly this text.

Offer: Tantra Enterprise, $9,999. Call 1: 30-minute Enterprise Evaluation Call on Cal.com with Conor or Scott (round robin). Promise: a written proposal within one business day of the call, carrying the closer's Call 2 link. Call 2: 30-minute Proposal Review with the same closer, where the decision is made (Adi, 2026-11-05). WhatsApp replaces SMS everywhere.

Rules for Every Sequence

  • Whatsapp hours: WhatsApp only between 08:00 and 20:00 in the lead's timezone (Cal.com attendee timezone, else the default_timezone setting). A step due outside the window waits for the next 08:00; a pre-call reminder that would land after the call starts is skipped.
  • Stop: Every sequence with stops_on_reply stops the moment the lead replies, books, or the deal changes stage. A and B are booking logistics: they stop on cancel, stage change past the call, or opt-out, not on a YES reply.
  • Opt out: STOP in any channel tags the contact Do Not Contact and cancels every pending step.
  • Optional lines: A line that starts with ? is dropped when one of its merge fields is empty; otherwise the ? is removed.
