27 Retargeting: Early Leavers and No-Shows, Six Weeks

Registrants who left before minute 25 or never showed get six weeks of email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn via Tantra and Meta and LinkedIn ads, then drop out. The script turns each WebinarJam export into hashed ad audiences. It reads segments only and never posts CRM events: item 17's import_attendance is the only producer of attendance events.

Early leavers (before minute 25) have now seen the initial pitch at minute 20 (deck slide 23: what Tantra Enterprise is, who it is for, $9,999, and the Evaluation Call, proposal and Proposal Review path; QR at minute 22), re-timed 2026-11-05.

How to Use It

Weekly, the day after the webinar: the four steps in RETARGETING_PLAN.md ("Ad Audiences: Weekly Refresh"). Real exports and outputs live in ~/tantra-private/27-retargeting/; the script refuses to write real-looking data inside the repo. Test: python3 deliverables/27-retargeting/test/test_retarget.py.

What Adi Must Fill

booking.round_robin_url, domains.funnel_domain, integrations.meta_pixel_id and linkedin_insight_partner_id (for the ad accounts), and a LinkedIn profile URL column in the CRM for 27-L1.

Notes for the Lead

  • The WebinarJam column names are an assumption (no real export seen). First real export: check summary.json "columns_matched" and edit webinarjam_columns.json.
  • Early leaver is measured by minutes in the room (contract), not the minute someone left; a late joiner who stayed 10 minutes counts as an early leaver.
  • LinkedIn upload format: the LinkedIn files carry one column, email, SHA-256 of the lowercase address. That is our reading of LinkedIn's contact-list spec, not yet tested: on the first upload, check Campaign Manager accepts it and reports a match rate; if not, switch to its current template.
  • Exclusions file: must include booked, replied, opted out, re-registered, won and lost; the script stops if it has no email column or no rows.
  • Ads use "one client" with no name; ad creatives need Adi's Kie.ai avatar (item 5 to 10 design or a new render).
