LinkedIn Outreach: Master Overview

Six senders, four campaign types, one webinar. Adi hosts; the other five invite on his behalf, each in their own role's voice. Every message points to the same registration page, tagged per sender and per campaign type so the CRM credits the right person.

Every set stops for a person the day they reply, register or ask to stop. Every sender has their own wording for every message: LinkedIn flags the same template sent from several accounts, so build.py fails if two senders share a message or its first 60 characters.

  • Ramp. Every account, Adi's included, starts at its tier's start value. Raise by 5 a day only after two clean weeks (no LinkedIn warning, acceptance steady), up to the tier's maximum. On any warning, go back to the last clean week.
  • Why 35 at most. 35 a weekday is 175 a week, under the 180 cap. The playbook's 75 a day would pass 180 by the third day.
  • Tiers. New: under 1 year old, under 300 connections, or a free plan. Established: 1 to 3 years, 300+ connections, Premium. Mature: 3+ years, 1,000+ connections, Sales Navigator. Until Adi confirms: Adi mature, everyone else new.
  • InMail credits. Sales Navigator 50 a month, Premium Business 15, Premium Career 5, free none (Tantra's LinkedIn page). Credits allow about 2 a day; the rest of the playbook's 30 must be Open Profile messages, which use no credits.
  • Event invites. Only if Adi creates a LinkedIn event for the webinar. None of the 24 sets needs one.

Audience Splits (No Prospect Hears From Two Profiles)

Each prospect has one owner profile, keyed on LinkedIn URL. dedupe.py applies the rules below and writes an owner_profile column (prospect files stay outside the repo; the script refuses to write inside it):

python3 deliverables/04-linkedin-templates/dedupe.py ~/tantra-prospects/owners.csv ~/tantra-prospects/list.csv --suppress ~/tantra-prospects/do-not-contact.csv

Then split the output by owner_profile and campaign, and import each part into that sender's campaign.

  1. 1st-degree connections (messenger). If several team members are connected to the same person, the one who messaged them most recently owns them. If nobody has, apply rule 3.
  2. Post engagers (content engagement). Engagers belong to the profile whose post they engaged with. If they engaged with several team posts, the earliest engagement wins. Anyone already owned by another profile is dropped from the export.
  3. Cold prospects (connector and InMail), by job title, first match wins:
Order Title contains Owner
1 CTO, VP or head of engineering, technical co-founder Bharath
2 CMO, VP or head of marketing, growth, demand generation Bhaskar
3 RevOps, marketing ops, sales ops, automation Aquila
4 COO, head of operations, chief of staff, general manager Nelbin
5 Head of digital, web or product; agency owner Ben
6 Founder, CEO, MD, owner, president (and anyone left) Adi
  1. One campaign at a time. A person in any active LinkedIn campaign, a registrant (CRM webinar.registered), anyone who replied and anyone on do-not-contact is removed from every other campaign's list.
  2. Connector and InMail never overlap. InMail goes to the best fits and Open Profiles. Connector takes the rest of that profile's audience.

Weekly Schedule

W is the webinar day (webinar.day_of_week in funnel-config.json, still FILL_). Sends run Monday to Friday in the audience's business hours.

When What runs
Every weekday Connector, at the profile's daily pace. The copy never names a date, so it runs every week without edits
Within 24 hours of each post A new content engagement campaign for that post (posts come from item 5)
W minus 6 to W minus 2 New messenger and InMail batches start, so the second touch lands before the session
W minus 1 and W No new batches. Follow-ups already scheduled keep running
Day after W Remove everyone who registered, from every list

Tantra Fit (Read From the Tantra App Source, Usetantra/tantra-frontend-v2, Clone of 2026-10-11)

  • Step actions. LinkedIn steps are visit_profile, connection_request and send_message (src/lib/channels/types.ts). Connector, messenger and content engagement map straight onto them. The CSV columns map to the step API used by the campaign kit: delay_days to delayDays, action to action, message to messageTemplate.
  • InMail is not a Tantra step. No InMail action exists in that source, so InMail step 1 ships separately in csv/inmail_manual_step1.csv and the sender sends it by hand in Sales Navigator. Tantra cannot see who replied, so 5 days later the sender imports only non-repliers into the 2-step follow-up campaign (csv/inmail_followup.csv). The Tantra CSVs hold only visit_profile, connection_request and send_message; build.py fails on anything else.
  • Engager lists. Tantra has no "people who engaged with this post" step. Each post's reactions and comments list has to be exported to a CSV outside the repo, with first name, LinkedIn URL, degree and post_topic, then imported with post_topic added as a custom field (Add Custom Field on import).
  • Merge tags. {{firstName}} and {{company}}, as documented on https://usetantra.com/help/templates-and-ai/merge-tags-and-spintax (read 2026-11-04). An unknown tag renders blank. {{custom.post_topic}} is a custom field; if it renders blank in the test message, type the phrase into that post's campaign instead. Before any launch, send one test message to an internal contact and check every tag filled.
  • Wait after a connection request. Whether Tantra counts the wait from the request or from acceptance is unverified (workshop launch kit). Check the first draft's timeline.
  • Slots. Each connected LinkedIn account uses one social account slot on the plan. The workshop launch kit read 0 LinkedIn accounts and 1 free slot (GrowthClub workspace, 2026-10-18). Six senders need six slots.

Adi Steps

  1. Confirm the six profiles. Open profiles.json and fill, for each person: linkedin_url, title (exactly as on their LinkedIn headline), linkedin_plan (free, premium_career, premium_business or sales_navigator) and account_tier (new, established or mature). Only Adi's URL was found in context: https://www.linkedin.com/in/adisuja (title in funnel-config: "Founder, Tantra"). After that, the lead session runs python3 deliverables/04-linkedin-templates/build.py and the limits in each profile file update.
  2. Decide on LinkedIn plans. InMail needs Sales Navigator (50 credits a month) or Premium (5 or 15). A free account gets only a few connection notes a month, so the connector and content engagement sets need Premium or Sales Navigator on every sender. Buying a plan is Adi's call, on each person's own LinkedIn account.
  3. Decide on Tantra slots. Open https://usetantra.com/connectors/channels/linkedin. If Connect account says an add-on is needed, extra slots are under https://usetantra.com/billing (button Activate in Billing). Adi decides how many senders to connect. Nothing was bought.
  4. Each sender connects their own account (about 5 minutes, their own login; nobody else signs in for them):
  5. Sign in at https://usetantra.com/sign-in.
  6. Open https://usetantra.com/connectors/channels/linkedin and click Connect account.
  7. On the Sign in tab, enter their LinkedIn Email or phone and Password. Under What this account has access to, tick LinkedIn, plus Sales Navigator if they have it.
  8. Click Connect LinkedIn and enter the code LinkedIn sends.
  9. When the row shows Connected, open the row's menu, choose Set daily and weekly sending limits and enter their tier's start numbers from that person's profile file.
  10. Afterwards: their account can be picked as the sender on that person's four draft campaigns.
  11. Send a test message first. Before starting any campaign, add an internal contact (a team member) to it, let one message go out, and check the name, company and link all filled. Then remove them and start.
  12. Fill the funnel domain. domains.funnel_domain in deliverables/_shared/funnel-config.json is still FILL_. Until it is filled, every link reads {{domains.funnel_domain}}/?utm_source=linkedin.... Once it is filled, the lead runs build.py and the CSVs are ready to load. Do not load them before then: Tantra would print the token as blank text.
