About (1,454 of 2,000 characters)

Most B2B teams do not have a lead problem. They have a leak problem. Marketing counts leads, sales counts deals, and nobody owns what happens in between.

Tantra builds the in between. We build AI revenue systems with B2B companies: the funnel, the outreach, the nurture and the pipeline, wired into one system with one owner and one set of numbers.

What we build with your team: - Traffic: cold email, cold calendar invites, LinkedIn and content, every link tagged by source. - Capture: registration and booking pages that feed one CRM. - Nurture: reminders and follow-ups by email and WhatsApp, timed to each buyer. - Conversion: a weekly live session that ends in booked calls. - Close: a sales pipeline where every booking, call and payment moves the deal on its own. - Growth: a year of follow-up, so the people who did not buy this month stay warm.

How we work: AI agents do the building inside written rules, and your team approves every send, launch and spend before it happens.

Our team has built funnels and outreach for 250+ brands in 47+ industries across 21+ countries, over 10+ years.

Two ways to work with us: - The Tantra platform: run outreach and nurture from your own mailboxes and accounts. - Tantra Enterprise: we build your funnel with you, coach your team for 3 months, and you get a year on the platform to run it.

Want to see the system working? Join our weekly live session: use the Register button at the top of this page.
