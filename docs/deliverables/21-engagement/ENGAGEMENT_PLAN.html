Engagement Plan: Polls, Chat Prompts, Offer Cues and the Path Into the CRM

People who type stay, and people who stay book. This is every interaction in the 60-minute session, timed to the deck (item 16, deliverables/16-deck/deck/index.html), and how each answer and click reaches the CRM sheet (CRM_CONTRACT.md).

Timing source: engagement.json, generated from the deck spec by node export_from_deck.mjs. Change the deck, rerun it, and the test (tests/test_map_engagement.py) fails until this page matches.

Who does what live: - Adi hosts alone: the only person on camera and the only voice. He asks every poll and prompt out loud and reads every question out in Q and A. There is no co-host. - Moderator: behind the scenes, never on camera or mic. In WebinarJam Basic they sit in the second presenter seat with the camera off, because Basic has no Moderator role (item 17). Launches polls and offers on cue, posts the lines below, answers chat, sends Adi the Q and A list in private chat.

Merge fields: {{booking.round_robin_url}} and the other {{...}} values come from funnel-config.json. The offer links add ?utm_source=webinarjam&utm_medium=live_offer&utm_content=<cue id>.

Polls (4)

Each poll runs twice at once: as a WebinarJam poll (the room sees the result) and as a chat prompt (the CRM gets each person's answer, because WebinarJam keeps poll results per session, not per person). Launch on the slide, close after 45 seconds, read the split aloud. A chat letter counts for a poll from the minute it opens until 3 minutes later.

From WebinarJam to the CRM Sheet

Everything posts to {{integrations.crm_webhook_url}}?k=<secret> as CRM_CONTRACT bodies. No other event names.

What Source Tool Event
Registration Registration page or calendar RSVP item 17 Worker and register.mjs webinar.registered
Attendance, minutes in room, no-shows WebinarJam registrants export ("Export all registrants") item 17 integration/import_attendance.mjs webinar.attended, webinar.no_show (the CRM sets early_leaver before minute 25, stayed_to_offer after)
Poll answers, per person WebinarJam chat log: letters typed in each poll's window map_engagement.py (this folder) webinar.poll_answered {poll_id, answer}
Offer clicks, per person Cal.com bookings carrying utm_content=<cue id>, plus a chat message that is only the word "BOOKED" after a cue ("not booked yet" never counts) map_engagement.py webinar.cta_clicked {cta_id}
Bookings Cal.com webhook (item 19) CRM web app booking.created

Why not webhooks: WebinarJam's custom webhook has its own payload, no signature, and fires "missed" and "left early" only after the session (item 17). WebinarJam cannot export poll answers or offer clicks per person. So the chat log and the booking UTM are the per-person record.

Poll answers in chat may be a letter ("B", "b)", "option B"), a letter with text ("D - follow-up") or the option text ("follow up"); "a great hour" is not an answer. Chat times may be offsets (00:04:40) or clock times (11:04:40, read as clock when it falls past the session length).

Matching people: a chat line with an email uses it. A line with only a name is matched to the registrants export; a name shared by two registrants is skipped, never guessed, and listed in unmatched.csv. Presenter names in columns.json are ignored.

After every session (Ops, T plus 2 hours)

  1. WebinarJam: the session's Log, download the chat; Registrants, this session, Export all registrants. Cal.com: export bookings created today (or use the CRM's booking rows) with utm_content. Save all three outside the repo, for example ~/Downloads/.
  2. Attendance (item 17): node deliverables/17-webinarjam/integration/import_attendance.mjs --csv ~/Downloads/<registrants>.csv --date <YYYY-MM-DD> --out /tmp/attendance.json, check, then again with --live.
  3. Polls and clicks, dry run: python3 -I deliverables/21-engagement/map_engagement.py --chat ~/Downloads/<chat>.csv --registrants ~/Downloads/<registrants>.csv --bookings ~/Downloads/<bookings>.csv --date <YYYY-MM-DD> --start <HH:MM> --tz <Area/City> --out ~/tantra-private/21-engagement/<YYYY-MM-DD> Read the printed counts and unmatched.csv. Then the same command with --send (needs integrations.crm_webhook_url filled and the secret in $CRM_SHARED_SECRET or ~/.config/tantra/keys/crm-shared-secret).
  4. Delete the three exports.

Verify before the first live session

  • The chat log download exists on our plan and has a time column and either an email or a name column. Put the real headers first in columns.json; set time_is to offset or clock.
  • Cal.com keeps utm_content from the booking link on the booking and in its export (item 19).
  • One test registration on example.com through the whole path: register, type a poll letter in chat, click an offer, book, run the two importers in dry run, and see the rows on the Webinar tab.
