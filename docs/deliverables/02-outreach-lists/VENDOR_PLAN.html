Vendor Sourcing Plan

Do We Need to Buy Rows for the 1M?

No. The master CSV gives 2,037,463 usable cold rows, so the 1,000,000-row main list and the 343,200 ramp invites come from it with no gap (SLICE_STATS.md).

We should still buy one list, for three reasons:

  1. No titles. The master cannot tell a founder from an analyst. A vendor list filtered by title and company size is the only way to aim at the $10,000+ buyer.
  2. Runway after S10. The reserve left after 16 April 2027 is 591,418 rows inside the domain cap, and 79% of the full reserve has no name in the address. The weeks after S10 need fresher rows.
  3. Fatigue. Every master row was invited once already by other senders, between 2026-07-22 and 2026-10-19.

Country: United States
Exclude: education, government, non-profit, healthcare providers, retail, hospitality

Format: one CSV, UTF-8.
