Item 2: Outreach Lists (1M Cold Calendar List)

The 1,000,000-row Enterprise cold calendar list is cut from the master CSV alone, with no gap. It sits in 10 weekly slices of 100,000 (8 Feb to 16 Apr 2027). Before them come 10 ramp weeks for the warm-up, then there is a warm list, a reserve and an EU/UK hold.

What Is Here

File What it is
ICP_AND_SEGMENTS.md The Enterprise buyer, tiers A, B and C, every exclusion and hold, and what the master CSV cannot tell us
slice_enterprise_lists.py Cuts the lists. Reads the master CSV path as an argument; writes prospect files only outside the repo
verify_slices.py Checks every written file independently (header, duplicates, filters, caps, 60-day re-invite gap, hashes). Last run: PASS
SLICE_STATS.md, slice-stats.json Count-only stats per slice, written by the script
SLICING_PLAN.md Which slice goes to which sender pool and week, dated from 2026-11-04, plus the weeks 1 to 3 team step
VENDOR_PLAN.md What to buy, from whom, at what price, and the brief to send
EVAL.md The done checklist, ticked

The lists themselves (names and emails) are in ~/tantra-enterprise-lists/ on Adi's Mac, folder mode 700, files mode 600, never in this repo: main/S01_P1.csv to S10_P4.csv, ramp/R01_* to R10_*, hold/, reserve/cold_reserve_after_S10.csv, MANIFEST.json (rows and SHA-256 per file).

The Numbers

  • Master: 2,752,479 rows. Cold usable after every filter: 2,037,463.
  • Main list: 1,000,000 (S01 to S10). Gap to 1M: 0.
  • Ramp weeks: 343,200 invites (18,927 warm, 324,273 cold). Reserve after S10: 591,418 rows inside the domain cap.
  • Re-invite gap: every row was invited by other senders between 2026-07-22 and 2026-10-19. No row goes into a week less than 60 days after its last invite; this only delays rows in R01 to R03.
  • Held until Adi checks EU, EEA, UK and Swiss outreach rules: 393,755. The hold is partial: country endings only, because location is empty.

Import Into Tantra

The CSV header is the list format in context/from-workshop-project/02-lists/lists.md section 4: first_name,last_name,title,company,website,email,linkedin_url,country. Tantra's importer (~/tantra-redesign/revops-os/os/kits/tantra/leads.mjs <file.csv> "<campaign>") maps these to its contact fields and detects a calendar campaign by itself. Rows with no known first name carry there, because the importer drops a row with a blank first name; calendar invites have no merge fields, so it is never shown. One draft campaign per file: SLICING_PLAN.md.

What Adi Fills or Decides

  • EU, EEA, UK and Swiss domains (393,755 rows): held, not sent, until the outreach rules for those countries are checked (lists.md, Outreach Rules by Country). To release a country, remove its ending from GDPR_HOLD in the script and rerun.
  • Day 0 (the Monday the four Workspaces are paid, item 3).
  • Weeks 1 to 3 internal activity: one team member per Workspace (SLICING_PLAN.md).
  • Vendor purchase: $630 at the quoted prices, Adi's step (VENDOR_PLAN.md).
  • Warm invite copy: the warm list gets the warm variant of the item 12 invite.
