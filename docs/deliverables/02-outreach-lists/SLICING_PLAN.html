The 10-Week, 100,000-a-Week Slicing Plan

Full volume cannot start on day one. Every sender Workspace sends no cold invites for three weeks, then 10 a sender a day, 25 from day 63 and 50 from day 91 (item 3, deliverables/03-outreach-infra/CAPACITY_PLAN.md). So the plan has two parts: 10 ramp weeks, then 10 main weeks at 100,000.

Assumptions Behind the Dates

  • Day 0 = Monday 9 November 2026: the day all four Workspaces have their $100 paid. Every date below moves by the same number of days if Day 0 moves (rerun the script with --day0).
  • 440 senders in 4 pools of 110 (P1 to P4, one Google Workspace each: W1 is the strategywebinars Workspace, W2 to W4 are new). Capacity at full volume is 110,000 a week; each main slice is 100,000.
  • 5 send days a week, Monday to Friday. No sends on 25 December 2026 or 1 January 2027.
  • Each week's invites are for the webinar of the following week ({{webinar.day_of_week}}, {{webinar.start_time_local}}; the event date is set in each Tantra campaign).

The Re-Invite Gap

Every row in the master was already invited by other senders between 2026-07-22 and 2026-10-19. The script puts no row into a week that starts less than 60 days after its last_invited_date, and ranks the oldest invites first. Rows still too recent when a week starts: R01 (30 Nov) 427,587; R02 254,513; R03 98,510; none from R04 (21 Dec) on. The main slices are not affected. Counts in SLICE_STATS.md, Re-invite gap.

Which Pool Sends Whom

Every recipient domain is tied to one pool by a fixed hash, so one company only hears from one Workspace. If a Workspace is suspended, only its pool's files wait; the other three keep sending.

Weeks 1 to 3: Internal Activity Only (team step)

No cold invites before day 21. In each of the first three weeks, every sender account does a little normal work so Google sees a real user:

  • Weeks 1 and 2: each sender accepts two or three internal calendar events a day sent by a teammate in the same Workspace, and joins one short Google Meet.
  • Week 3: each sender invites three to five known external contacts a day (partners, clients, team members' own work addresses) who accept.

Who does it: Adi names one team member per Workspace. Nothing is sent to the list.

Each Week, in Tantra (Adi or the team, never an agent)

  1. One calendar campaign per file, built as a draft (POST /campaigns/draft, type smart_calendar), senders = that pool's 110 addresses from deliverables/03-outreach-infra/sender-roster.csv.
  2. Import the file with node kits/tantra/leads.mjs <file.csv> "<campaign name>" (from ~/tantra-redesign/revops-os/os; it detects a calendar campaign by itself), or the CSV upload in Tantra. The header is already in Tantra's list format.
  3. Check every sender's daily limit matches the week in the roster before launch.
  4. Launch only with Adi's approval. Pausing cannot recall invites already sent.

When to Stop or Slow a Pool

Pause the pool and hold its files for a week if, in any send day: senders show Disconnected in Tantra, Google shows a sending-limit warning on any sender, or the pool's declines run well above the earlier weeks. Resume at the previous week's daily cap.
