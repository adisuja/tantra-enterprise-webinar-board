From Live Viewers to Sales Calls

Hi {{first_name}},

The Leak

The webinar ends. The room empties. And for most companies, the follow-up ends there too: one replay email to everyone, then silence.

But the people who stayed to the end, the people who left early and the people who never showed up are three different conversations. Send them all the same email and you lose most of all three.

The Fix: Three Tracks, Fourteen Days

  1. Stayed to the offer. They saw everything. Send the booking link and the recording within two hours, then answer the questions that stop people booking: is it for my size, how much of my time, do we start over.
  2. Left early. They missed the part that matters most. Send them to the exact minute they left.
  3. Never showed. Recording first. Give them one question to watch with: where do my leads go quiet?

Every track stops the moment someone books, replies or opts out. And when someone books, a person on your team confirms the call within five minutes.

The Proof

On Growth Idea's biggest webinar with us, about 8 of every 100 people who came live booked a sales call.

Across the first three episodes of FintechCircle's series, 17 calls were booked.

Do This This Week

  • Split your last webinar's registrants into stayed, left early and never showed.
  • Write one message for each group, with one link each.
  • Time how long your team takes to confirm a new booking.
