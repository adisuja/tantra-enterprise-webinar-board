Beehiiv Newsletter Setup: The Tantra AI Brief

The newsletter is where webinar registrants go after the 14-day post-webinar tracks. One issue a week, each teaching one part of the system, with one CTA. Nothing here is sent or changed by this repo: Adi does the clicks below.

3. Issue Sections (every issue, in this order)

  1. The Leak. A pain the reader recognises, in 2 to 4 short paragraphs.
  2. The Fix. One part of the system, step by step.
  3. The Proof. One row from PROOF_PACK.md, named client at most once.
  4. Do This This Week. One action, three bullets at most.
  5. One CTA. One button: the recording page or the evaluation call. Never both.

Subject lines: plain, specific, under 50 characters, no emoji. Every issue passes scan.py before it goes into Beehiiv.

4. Who Gets Added, and When (Consent)

  • Only registrants who ticked the newsletter box on the registration form. Box unticked by default, text: "Send me Adi's weekly newsletter. Unsubscribe any time." The webinar.registered event carries it as newsletter_opt_in (see README, contract note).
  • When: stayed_to_offer on day 15 after their webinar (after the item 22 track ends); early_leaver and no_show on day 42, the day they drop out of item 27 retargeting. The newsletter never stacks on a nurture or retargeting sequence. A registrant who books a call still gets added on their day.
  • Never: anyone tagged Do Not Contact, anyone who unsubscribed from Beehiiv before (reactivate_existing: false), anyone without the tick.
  • Unsubscribes flow back: a Beehiiv unsubscribe posts contact.opted_out with channel newsletter to the CRM webhook (Beehiiv webhook subscription.deleted, pointed at integrations.crm_webhook_url).

5. How Registrants Are Added

Every week (API, done by the item 23 runner): POST https://api.beehiiv.com/v2/publications/{BEEHIIV_PUBLICATION_ID}/subscriptions with header Authorization: Bearer <key from ~/.config/tantra/keys/> and body:

{
  "email": "sam@example.com",
  "reactivate_existing": false,
  "send_welcome_email": true,
  "double_opt_override": "off",
  "utm_source": "webinar",
  "utm_medium": "registration",
  "utm_campaign": "tew-2026-11-12",
  "custom_fields": [
    {"name": "first_name", "value": "Sam"},
    {"name": "company", "value": "Example Co"},
    {"name": "webinar_date", "value": "2026-11-12"},
    {"name": "webinar_segment", "value": "no_show"},
    {"name": "consent_source", "value": "registration_form"},
    {"name": "consent_at", "value": "2026-11-05T10:02:11Z"}
  ]
}

Field names verified against Beehiiv's docs in the June 2026 handoff. Custom fields must exist first (section 2).

One-off backfill (CSV import): export from the Contacts and Webinar tabs only the rows with newsletter_opt_in true, into the columns of import-template.csv. Keep the file outside the repo. In Beehiiv, import it, map each column to the custom field of the same name and email to Email, and turn the welcome email on. UTM values cannot be set by CSV, so the backfill carries consent_source instead.
