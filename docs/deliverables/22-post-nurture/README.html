22 Post-Webinar Nurture (WhatsApp, Email, Beehiiv)

Fourteen days of follow-up for three segments, pushing the Enterprise Evaluation Call and the recording page (Track A, who saw the offer, gets three teaching emails with no booking link and WhatsApp only on days 0, 3, 7 and 14), then the Beehiiv newsletter.

What Is Here

How to Use It

  • The tracks read the segment the CRM sets from item 17's import_attendance (webinar.attended or webinar.no_show).
  • Merge fields: as 15b, plus recording_url (the recording page) and recording_offer_url (the recording page starting at the minute the offer begins). Set that minute once the first recording exists.
  • WhatsApp templates here are follow-ups about Tantra: submit as category Marketing.
  • Newsletter issues: replace {{recording_url}} or {{booking_url}} with the real link when pasting into Beehiiv. Issues and the welcome email say "30-minute" and "Adi" from booking.duration_minutes and brand.host_name: change them by hand if those change.

What Adi Must Fill

booking.round_robin_url; domains.funnel_domain (recording page); integrations.beehiiv_publication_url; the Beehiiv sender mailbox; brand.legal_footer_address.

Notes for the Lead

  • WhatsApp keeps to 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone on every step, day 0 included (CRM_CONTRACT).
  • Track A ends on day 14 and joins the newsletter on day 15. Tracks B and C continue in item 27 and join the newsletter on day 42.
