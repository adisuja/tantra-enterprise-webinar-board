22 Post-Webinar Tracks: 14 Days, Three Segments

Attendance from WebinarJam (posted as webinar.attended or webinar.no_show by item 17's import_attendance, the only producer) puts each registrant into one segment (CRM_CONTRACT.md). Each segment gets its own track. Every message carries the recording page ({{recording_url}}, or {{recording_offer_url}} that starts at the offer), and every message except Track A's three teaching emails carries the Enterprise Evaluation Call link ({{booking_url}}). Track A already sat through the offer, so it gets no hard sell: WhatsApp only on days 0, 3, 7 and 14, and the day 1, 5 and 10 emails teach. Full copy: COPY.md. Import files: email.csv, whatsapp.csv.

Tracks B and C: each step is one email and one WhatsApp message, sent together. Track A: WhatsApp on 22A-0, 22A-3, 22A-5 and 22A-7 only. Day-level steps go at the webinar's clock time, moved into 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone (data.timezone, fallback webinar.timezone) with the same rule as 15b (scheduler.py rule 6).

Timing Rules

  • Day 0 starts when attendance arrives, not at the session's end. WebinarJam data is imported from T+120 min (item 17 import_attendance). If nothing has arrived by T+180 min, everyone not marked attended goes to track C.
  • WhatsApp only to whatsapp_opt_in true, and only 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone (CRM_CONTRACT). This includes the day 0 messages 22A-0, 22B-0 and 22C-0: when attendance lands outside the window, the email goes and the WhatsApp is skipped. These are follow-ups about Tantra, so submit them as Marketing templates (15b reminders are Utility).
  • Every email has the unsubscribe link and postal address; every WhatsApp ends "Reply STOP to opt out."

Exits (checked before every send)

Event (CRM_CONTRACT.md) Effect

Hand-Offs

  • Track A, day 15: into the Beehiiv newsletter if newsletter_opt_in (beehiiv/BEEHIIV_SETUP.md).
  • Tracks B and C, day 15: into item 27 (weeks 3 to 6 of retargeting; ads run from day 0, see deliverables/27-retargeting/RETARGETING_PLAN.md). Newsletter on day 42, when retargeting ends, if newsletter_opt_in, so the two never stack.
