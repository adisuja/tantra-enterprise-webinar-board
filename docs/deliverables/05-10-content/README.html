Adi's Decisions (logged for the lead)

  1. Picks: 5 of 10 in each of the 10 batches (3 carousels + 2 animated).
  2. The content-rule question: RULES.md says every post teaches the system and promotes the RevOps workshop series, never Tantra features. May posts in this funnel promote this webinar and Tantra Enterprise, given the rule to teach the system and not Tantra features? Until Adi answers, every idea teaches one stage of the system and puts only the webinar registration link in the first comment (or self-reply, or bio link). No idea names Tantra Enterprise or its price.
  3. The seventh batch: the brief says "7 batches ... for Adi, Aquila, Nelbin, Bharath, Bhaskar, Ben ... and 5 pieces (1 more batch) for the Tantra company page". These files read it as 6 people + 1 company page = 7. If Adi meant 7 person batches plus the company page (8 in all), a seventh person is missing from the brief: name them and a batch follows.
  4. Avatars: only Adi has an avatar library (~/tantra-content/content/brand/people/adi/avatars/). Aquila, Nelbin, Bharath, Bhaskar and Ben need 4 to 8 photos each from Adi (with their consent) before Kie.ai can make their avatars. Ideas that need a face say so in the INDEX; each also has a no-face or name-line fallback (name line only, no photo). Adi's AD1 to AD3 need 4 new poses.
  5. Team voices: no recorded stories exist for the five team members, so their ideas are method-led (Adi's voice rule asks for a story with a time anchor). One or two true stories from each person would let their posts open on a story.
  6. Optional story hooks: AD4 and FB5 carry an "optional story hook" that is only usable if Adi confirms it happened; the main hooks do not depend on it.
  7. Instagram account: Adi's own or the Tantra brand account (written for Adi; switches to we/you if Tantra).
  8. Team roles used to split topics (from the 2026-09-27 roster): Aquila automation and delivery, Nelbin operations, Bharath CTO, Bhaskar CMO, Ben development. Confirm each matches their LinkedIn headline (LinkedIn ranks posts that match the author's topics).
