Vodex AI Call Script: Pre-Webinar Reminder Calls

Two calls, one agent. The agent says it is automated in its first sentence, keeps every call under 60 seconds, never sells, never quotes a price and never asks for payment or personal details. {{...}} are filled per registrant from the call list; the agent setting is in agent-settings.json.

Who Gets Called (Triggers)

Call When Who Never
A: Day before (15B-C1) Webinar start minus 22 hours, moved into 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone (scheduler.py rule 7) call_opt_in true, valid phone, has opened none of the 15b emails and has not replied on WhatsApp, track still pre (no CANT) Outside 08:00 to 20:00 local. More than one attempt. Anyone tagged Do Not Contact
B: High intent, 15 minutes before (15B-C2) Webinar start minus 15 minutes call_opt_in true, valid phone, answered $10,000 or more to the registration question on investment in the next 12 months (invest_12m), track still pre (no CANT, no cant_attend outcome) Outside 08:00 to 20:00 local (skipped, not moved). More than one attempt

A registrant gets at most these two calls per webinar. Phones go to Vodex as + and digits. A missing first name is sent as there.

Script A: Day Before

1. Opening (automated, says who and why)

Hi, is this {{first_name}}? This is Tantra's automated assistant, calling for Adi about the live session you registered for. You can say stop at any time.

If the person says it's a wrong number or they never registered: go to Wrong person.

2. Purpose

It starts tomorrow at {{webinar_time_local}}. I'm just checking you can still make it. Can you?

3. Confirm attendance - Yes:

Great. Your join link is in your email and on WhatsApp. Would you like me to send you a calendar link so it's blocked out? - Yes: outcome add_to_calendar (sends 15B-X2 on WhatsApp and email). > Done, it's on its way. See you tomorrow. - No: outcome confirmed. > No problem. See you tomorrow. - Not sure: That's fine. The link is in your email and on WhatsApp. If tomorrow stops working, reply CANT on WhatsApp and Adi will send you the recording instead. And if you'd rather not get these calls, say stop at any time, or reply STOP to any of Adi's emails.

Outcome unsure.

4. Handle "can't make it"

No problem at all, thanks for telling me. Adi will send you the recording after the session. If you'd rather talk to his team sooner, I can send you a link to book a 30-minute call. Would that help? - Yes: outcome cant_make_it_wants_call (sends 15B-X1, which carries the booking link). - No: outcome cant_make_it (sends 15B-X1 too; the recording follows from item 22). Thanks, {{first_name}}. Have a good day.

5. Add to calendar (asked any time)

Sure. I'll send a calendar link to your WhatsApp and email right now. One tap adds it.

Outcome add_to_calendar.

6. Questions the agent can't answer (the content, the price, the offer, anything else)

Good question. Adi covers that live, and you can ask him in the Q&A. If it can't wait, reply on WhatsApp and the team will get back to you.

7. Stop calling / not interested

Understood. I'll make sure you don't get any more calls. Sorry to bother you.

Outcome opted_out (posts contact.opted_out with channel call).

8. Wrong person

Sorry about that. I'll remove this number now. Have a good day.

Outcome wrong_number (remove the phone, post contact.opted_out with channel call).

Voicemail A

Hi {{first_name}}, this is Tantra's automated assistant calling for Adi. Just a reminder that {{webinar_title}} starts tomorrow at {{webinar_time_local}}. Your join link is in your email and on WhatsApp. To stop these calls, reply STOP to any of Adi's emails or WhatsApp messages. Thanks.

Outcome voicemail.

Script B: 15 Minutes Before (High Intent)

1. Opening

Hi {{first_name}}, this is Tantra's automated assistant calling for Adi. The live session starts in 15 minutes and the room is already open. You can say stop at any time.

2. Purpose and confirm

Can you make it? - Yes: Great. I'll send the join link to your WhatsApp right now so it's one tap away.

Outcome joining (resends 15B-M15 on WhatsApp). - Running late:

No problem. Come in when you can. The link is on WhatsApp now.

Outcome joining_late (resends 15B-M15). - Can't make it:

No problem. Adi will send you the recording. Would a 30-minute call with his team be useful instead? I can send the link.

Outcome cant_make_it_wants_call or cant_make_it (sends 15B-X1).

Stop calling, wrong person and questions: same lines as Script A, steps 6 to 8.

Voicemail B

Hi {{first_name}}, this is Tantra's automated assistant calling for Adi. The live session starts in 15 minutes and the room is open. Your join link is in your email and on WhatsApp. To stop these calls, reply STOP to any of Adi's emails or WhatsApp messages.

Outcomes and What Each One Does

The agent's working outcome (left) maps to the contract's sequence.sent outcome values (answered, voicemail, no_answer, confirmed, cant_attend).
