15b Sequence Map: Registration to the Live Room

The webinar runs weekly on WebinarJam and people register any day before it. So every step is timed from webinar start (T), except the confirmation and the catch-up, which are timed from registration. Copy for every step: COPY.md. Import files: email.csv, whatsapp.csv, vodex.csv. The rules below are also code: scheduler.py (--selftest runs 10 registration cases and the WhatsApp window checks).

Who Gets What

  • Email: every registrant, from 15B-R1 on (the confirmation email is WebinarJam's). Each email carries the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
  • Join link: {{join_url}} merges from the contact: the personal WebinarJam link the register Worker adds to webinar.registered. The generic room link (webinar.join_url) is a fallback only.
  • WhatsApp: only whatsapp_opt_in true (the registration tick box), and only 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone (see Sending Hours). Every message ends "Reply STOP to opt out." STOP in any channel posts contact.opted_out and ends every sequence.
  • Template categories: Utility for every reminder; Marketing for 15B-D3 (client proof) and 15B-X1 (evaluation call link).
  • Calls: only registrants who ticked the call box (call_opt_in, see README contract note), with a valid phone, inside 08:00 to 20:00 in their own timezone. At most two calls per webinar.
  • Quick replies (YES, CAL, CANT) post contact.quick_reply (CRM_CONTRACT): they do not stop the reminders. CAL sends X2. CANT moves the contact to the replay track and sends X1. Any other reply goes to a person (posts contact.replied).
  • Calls offer a way out on every call: "say stop at any time, or reply STOP to any of Adi's emails."

Late Registrants

  1. Registered after the start: only 15B-L0 (we're live) goes out, at once, and only while the session is still running (before start + webinar.duration_minutes). Registered after it ended: nothing from 15b; the item 22 no-show track or the next date applies.
  2. Past steps are skipped. Registered 4 days before? W1 and V2 never send.
  3. Missed the first video email? 15B-R1 sends all four videos as one page, 60 minutes after registering, if that is still 26 hours or more before T.
  4. No pile-ups. A step due less than 30 minutes after another planned message is skipped. Exceptions: 15 minutes, live now and late +10, because they carry the link when it matters. Example: registered at T minus 3h10m gets the confirmation, then not the 3-hour reminder 10 minutes later.

Sending Hours

The registrant's timezone is timezone on webinar.registered (IANA, from the registration page's browser). When it is missing, the webinar timezone (webinar.timezone) is used.

  • WhatsApp, every step, live-day reminders included: only 08:00 to 20:00 in the registrant's timezone (CRM_CONTRACT). Outside the window the email still goes and the WhatsApp is skipped. Waiving this for live-day steps is Adi's decision.
  • Day-level steps (W1, V2, D3, V4, D1) move into the window, both channels: before 08:00 moves to 08:00, from 20:00 moves to 19:00, same local date.
  • Event-hour steps (H3, H1, M15, L0, L10) go at their time by email; their WhatsApp goes only if that time is inside the window.
  • Calls always keep to 08:00 to 20:00 local. C1 moves into the window or is skipped; C2 is skipped.

Exits and Changes

  • Opted out (any channel): every step stops.
  • Track moved to replay (CANT quick reply or call outcome cant_attend): D1 to L10 and both calls stop; the item 22 no-show track still sends the recording.
  • Re-registered for a later date: pending steps for the old date are cancelled and the plan is rebuilt for the new date.
  • Booked an evaluation call before the webinar: reminders continue (they should still attend).

Building It in Tantra

Same pattern as context/from-workshop-project/15b-pre-nurture/nurture.md: a Tantra step waits whole days, so each timed message is its own campaign (email step plus WhatsApp step, both at wait 0), keyed by template_name. The runner (item 23) adds each registrant to the campaign at the time scheduler.py gives, with the per-person fields in the variables column as Tantra custom fields. Campaign schedule open every day, every hour, in the webinar's timezone.
