Adi's Steps: Item 3, Cold Calendar Senders

Everything an agent could do is done: the plan, 90 checked names, every DNS record, the roster. These steps need your sign-in or your card. Do them in order. When a step is done, tell the build agent "step N done" (and the dates asked for) and it carries on.

Files you need open: DNS_RECORDS.md (records to paste), sender-roster.csv (users), DOMAIN_SHORTLIST.md (names and which Workspace each goes to). Decide first: 80 domains (the default, CAPACITY_PLAN.md) or your floor of 20. The steps are the same; only the count changes.

Step 1. The strategywebinars Workspace (W1): domain, plan, $100

  1. Open https://admin.google.com and sign in with the login in ~/.config/tantra/keys/strategywebinars-workspace.env. It signs in on a Google temporary domain (...appstempdomain.goog).
  2. Open https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/subscriptions. If the subscription says Trial, click it, then End trial (or Set up billing). Pick Business Starter, Flexible Plan.
  3. Open https://admin.google.com/ac/domains/manage. If strategywebinars.com is not listed: click Add a domain, type strategywebinars.com in Domain name, choose Secondary domain, click Add domain and start verification. Copy the TXT value Google shows.
  4. strategywebinars.com's DNS is at Cloudflare (registered there 2026-09-27). Open https://dash.cloudflare.com, click strategywebinars.com, DNS, Records, Add record: Type TXT, Name @, Content the value from 1.3, Save. Back in Google, click Verify my domain.
  5. On https://admin.google.com/ac/domains/manage, next to strategywebinars.com click Make primary. Google may ask to set up Gmail MX first: add MX @ SMTP.GOOGLE.COM priority 1 at Cloudflare the same way.
  6. Rename the admin off the temporary domain: https://admin.google.com/ac/users, click the admin user, User information, Primary email, choose @strategywebinars.com, Save. Update the login file yourself.
  7. Pay $100: https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/accounts, click the payment account, Make a payment, Amount 100 (USD), Make a payment. The 60-day clock starts today.
  8. Open https://usetantra.com, Accounts, and search each of: strategywebinars, visionarywebinars, seminarvision, nextgenwebinars, signonline. Count how many show Connected.

Tell the agent: the W1 payment date and the Connected count.

Step 2. Register the domains at iFreeDomains

  1. Open https://ifreedomains.com/register.php. Fill First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Address, City, State, Postcode, Country with your real details (the registry requires them), set a Password, click Register.
  2. Open https://ifreedomains.com/cart.php?a=add&domain=register. Type the first name from DOMAIN_SHORTLIST.md (northgatesessions.com), click Check, Add to Cart on the $0.00 result, Checkout, Complete Order.
  3. Click the ICANN registrant verification email. The registry emails the registrant address once per registrant contact (one email covers every domain on that contact; a second iFreeDomains account means a second email). Check spam. It must be clicked within 15 days of registration, or the registry suspends every domain on that contact.
  4. Open https://lookup.icann.org, type northgatesessions.com, click Lookup. It must show a registrar and today's date. If not, stop and tell the agent (the fallback is buying at Cloudflare).
  5. Repeat 2.2 for the other names. ".com" is marked Limited at iFreeDomains: if the cart asks for payment or refuses another free .com, open another account at https://ifreedomains.com/register.php with another email (and click its ICANN email, 2.3). If a name is taken or priced as "Special", skip it and tell the agent which.
  6. For each domain: https://ifreedomains.com/clientarea.php?action=domains, Manage Domain, Management Tools, Nameservers, choose Use default nameservers, Change Nameservers.
  7. Same page, once the funnel exists: Management Tools, URL Forwarding, paste the registration page (domains.funnel_domain in deliverables/_shared/funnel-config.json, still FILL), choose Redirect (301), Set URL.
  8. Same domain, Manage iFreeDomains DNS: add the MX, SPF and DMARC lines from its block in DNS_RECORDS.md (Name @ means leave Name blank; MX Priority 1). The two FILL lines come in steps 3, 4 and 6.

Step 3. Create Workspaces W2, W3 and W4

Three times: W2 on ridgewayinsights.com, W3 on larkspurinsights.com, W4 on juniperhillinsights.com.

  1. Open https://workspace.google.com/business/signup/welcome. Business name: your company's legal name (as on W1's billing). Number of employees: 2 to 9. Region: your country. Next.
  2. Your contact details and current email, Next.
  3. Does your business have a domain?: Yes, I have one I can use, type the primary domain, Next, then Next on Use this domain to set up the account.
  4. How you'll sign in: username admin, a new password (keep it in your password manager, never in this repo or a chat), Agree and continue.
  5. Pick Business Starter, add your card, finish checkout.
  6. Google shows a TXT verification value: add it at iFreeDomains (Manage iFreeDomains DNS, TXT, Name blank, Value as shown), then click Verify in Google.
  7. End the trial: https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/subscriptions, click the subscription, End trial (or Set up billing), pick Business Starter, Flexible Plan. A trial keeps Google's lowest limits however much you prepay.
  8. Pay $100: https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/accounts, the payment account, Make a payment, Amount 100, Make a payment.

Tell the agent the payment date of each Workspace. It writes it into warmup_start_day0 and re-dates the plan.

Step 4. Add the other domains to each Workspace

Signed in to the right Workspace (the Workspace column of DOMAIN_SHORTLIST.md; reserves wait):

  1. https://admin.google.com/ac/domains/manage, Add a domain, type the domain in Domain name, choose Secondary domain, Add domain and start verification.
  2. Paste the TXT value Google shows into that domain's DNS at iFreeDomains (first line of its block in DNS_RECORDS.md), then Verify my domain. If it does not show Verified, wait 30 minutes and click again.
  3. If Google shows Activate Gmail, click it: the MX record is already in place.

Step 5. DMARC alias per domain

https://admin.google.com/ac/users, click the admin user (in W1, do step 1.6 first so the admin is on strategywebinars.com, not the temporary domain), User information, Alternate email addresses (email alias), Add an alternate email, type dmarc, pick the domain, Save. One alias per sending domain.

Step 6. DKIM for every sending domain

  1. https://admin.google.com/ac/apps/gmail/authenticateemail.
  2. Pick the domain in Selected domain, Generate new record, keep DKIM key bit length 2048 and Prefix selector google, Generate.
  3. Copy the TXT record value into that domain's DNS at iFreeDomains: TXT, Name google._domainkey, Value as copied.
  4. After 30 minutes, same page, Start authentication. If it errors, wait and click again.

Step 7. Create the senders

110 per Workspace, already named in sender-roster.csv (every sender is you, in six address forms; swap in team members only if you add their last names).

  1. https://admin.google.com/ac/users, Bulk update users, Download blank CSV template.
  2. One row per sender of that Workspace: First Name, Last Name (from display_name), Email Address (email), Password (new, yours), Org Unit Path /.
  3. Attach CSV file, pick the file, Upload. Google emails you when the users exist.
  4. Put the creation date in the created column.

Step 8. Connect the senders to Tantra

One service account per sender (google-workspace.md, Owner clicks C4 to C6), two Google Cloud projects per Workspace (service_account_project in the roster).

  1. Fastest: the onboarding centre's Build my service accounts button, signed in once as each Workspace's super admin.
  2. By hand: https://console.cloud.google.com, create the project named in the roster, enable Gmail API and Google Calendar API, then IAM and Admin, Service Accounts, Create service account, open it, Keys, Add key, Create new key, JSON. Upload each file at https://usetantra.com, Connectors, Google Service Accounts, Upload Service Accounts, Upload. Never put a key file in this repo or a chat.
  3. Authorize each: https://admin.google.com/ac/owl/domainwidedelegation, Add new, paste the numeric Client ID, paste this in OAuth scopes, Authorize:
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile,https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify
  1. https://usetantra.com, Accounts, Verify and Add Accounts, paste the Workspace's 110 sender addresses (one per line), Verify & Add. Each must show Connected; "unauthorized_client" means the email was pasted instead of the numeric Client ID.
  2. Mark tantra_service_account_connected yes in the roster.

Step 9. Weeks 1 to 3: internal activity, then daily limits every Monday

  1. Weeks 1 to 3: the team step in deliverables/02-outreach-lists/SLICING_PLAN.md (internal events, then invites to known contacts). No list sends.
  2. Every Monday from week 4: in https://usetantra.com, Accounts, open each sender and set its daily invite limit to the roster's number for that week (daily_cap_wk01 to daily_cap_wk14_on): 0 in weeks 1 to 3, 10 in weeks 4 to 9, 25 in weeks 10 to 13, 50 from week 14. With your written OK, the build agent can set them through the Tantra API instead (PATCH /accounts/<email> with maxPerDay).

What Happens Next

With every sender Connected and the payment dates in hand, the agent re-dates SLICING_PLAN.md (--day0) and the first warm-list week (R01) can be built as Tantra drafts. You approve each launch.
