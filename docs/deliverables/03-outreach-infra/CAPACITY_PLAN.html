Capacity Plan: Up to 100,000 Calendar Invites a Week

The Short Answer

440 sender accounts on 80 new domains in 4 Google Workspaces send 100,000 invites a week, with 10% spare. They cannot do it on day one. A new Workspace sends no cold invites for its first three weeks, then a few a day until day 90. If the four Workspaces are paid and set up by Monday 9 November 2026, the first 100,000 week is 8 to 12 February 2027. The 10 full weeks run to 16 April 2027.

The Maths

Invites a sender a week from day 91      = 50 a day x 5 send days            = 250
Senders for 100,000 a week               = 100,000 / 250                      = 400
Senders planned (10% spare)              = 440                                -> capacity 110,000 a week
Domains                                  = 80 active (20 per Workspace) + 10 parked reserves
Senders per domain                       = 6 on 40 domains, 5 on 40 domains   = 440
Invites per domain a week (sending 100k) = 100,000 / 80                       = 1,250 (1,375 at full capacity)
Workspaces                               = 4 (W1 strategywebinars + 3 new), 110 senders and 20 domains each

Why spare capacity: a sender that shows Disconnected or gets a Google warning sits out its week; with 440, losing 40 senders still sends 100,000.

Why 4 Workspaces: Google suspends a Workspace, not a user. With 4, one suspension stops a quarter of the volume. Each Workspace uses two Google Cloud projects (55 and 55 service accounts), under the default of 100 service accounts per project.

Per-Domain Load Against the E150 to E162 Exports

In references/cold-calendar-exports-E150-E162-stats.md, "Invites per domain" is invites sent divided by sender domains for one weekly event (E150 Pt.1: 106,532 / 74 = 1,440), spread over 5 send days. So the senders behind the master list ran about 1,440 invites per domain a week (about 288 a send day), over about 150 sender domains per weekly event across their two pools.

The Faster Alternative: 800 Senders

800 senders at 25 a day send 100,000 a week from day 63: first full week 11 to 15 January 2027, four weeks sooner. It needs 803 seats instead of 443 (about $6,745 a month on the Flexible plan against about $3,721), 160 domains at 5 senders each to keep the per-domain load, and 800 service accounts. From day 91 half the seats can be removed on the Flexible plan.

What could start sooner either way: the five older Tantra calendar senders (strategywebinars, visionarywebinars, seminarvision, nextgenwebinars, signonline) may add capacity in weeks 4 to 13 if they show Connected in Tantra (Adi step 1).
