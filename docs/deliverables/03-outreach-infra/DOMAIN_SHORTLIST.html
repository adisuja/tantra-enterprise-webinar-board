Domain Shortlist: 80 Active and 10 Reserves

Rules: .com, no hyphens, never growthclub.org or usetantra.com, nothing that reads as Tantra or GrowthClub, and none of the existing Tantra calendar names (strategywebinars, visionarywebinars, seminarvision, nextgenwebinars, signonline).

Why most names are plain. The senders behind the master list used webinar-themed domains, and 156,018 of those recipients declined. A plain, company-like name (northgatesessions.com) does not repeat that pattern. Each Workspace gets 15 plain names (pairs share a name, as one firm with two domains would) and 5 themed ones, so the two styles can be compared on decline rates in the first weeks.

Every name returned "not found" from the Verisign .com registry (RDAP) on 2026-11-04. A name can still be taken before Adi registers it, or be priced as "Special" at iFreeDomains: skip it and take a reserve. The plain names were checked for registration only: before buying, search each one to make sure no live company trades under it.
