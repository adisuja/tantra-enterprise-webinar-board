Estimate (ESTIMATED)

Monthly profit before tax ran at about £76K (FY to Mar 2023), £59K (FY to Mar 2024) and £37K (FY to Mar 2025): a falling trend going into the engagement. Revenue is profit plus costs, worked three ways for the year the engagement sat in:

  1. Cost build-up. Profit about £37K a month (assume flat to slightly lower in the year to Mar 2026). Staff of 6 at £40K to £65K loaded each is £20K to £33K a month. Contract coaches, marketing (including the outreach we ran), events, office and software: £8K to £35K a month. Total: about £65K to £105K a month.
  2. Margin benchmark. At the franchisor's 70% top-performer margin (G12), £37K profit implies £53K revenue. At a 30% to 35% consulting margin it implies £106K to £123K.
  3. History. Over £1M a year (about £83K a month) was published for 2016 and 2018 (G11), and profit was higher in 2021 to 2024 than in 2025.

Range: £60K to £125K a month, about $80K to $168K. Central: about £85K, about $114K. The 4 clients won (G13, G14) add roughly £8K to £10K a month of contracted value if each paid about £25K over a year; that is inside the range, not on top of it.

Stage

Scale, at the bottom edge. The central estimate sits just above $100K; the low end of the range is in Growth. If the year to 31 Mar 2026 accounts (due by 31 Dec 2026) show profit falling again, move it to Growth. At a 1.30 rate the central estimate is $111K (still Scale); at 1.38 it is $117K.

Confidence: medium that monthly revenue was between $80K and $170K; low on which side of the $100K line. It is clearly not Early and almost certainly not Large (6 staff, £37K monthly profit).

Estimate (ESTIMATED)

Two parts, for Mar to May 2026:

  1. Base business before the Board Programme. A 2-person company at about break-even (F5, F6) earns roughly what it spends: 2 staff at £30K to £70K loaded each (£60K to £140K a year) plus freelancers, events and platforms (£30K to £120K a year). That is £90K to £260K a year, about £7.5K to £22K a month. Debtors of £12K to £32K at year end fit that scale.
  2. Board Programme sales. 88 approved applicants in the window (F12). If 10% to 40% paid £1,950 (F9), that is 9 to 35 enrolments, £17K to £68K over three months, about £6K to £23K a month. Capacity of 25 per monthly Flagship cohort (F10) caps this at about £49K a month even at full fill.

Total: £13K to £45K a month, about $17K to $60K. Central: about £25K, about $34K. (That central figure matching the deck's ~$34K modelled pipeline is a coincidence; the pipeline number was not used.)

Stage

Growth, at the low end. The central estimate is $34K. Early (under $25K) is a real possibility if few approved applicants paid; Scale is ruled out by the filed scale of the company. At 1.30 the central is $33K; at 1.38 it is $35K: no change.

Confidence: low to medium. The weakest link is the paid conversion of approved applicants, which no source records. One question to Susanne (how many paid for the April and May cohorts) would settle it.

What would change these calls

  • Growth Idea: its accounts for the year to 31 Mar 2026 (due by 31 Dec 2026). Profit well below £37K a month would point to Growth.
  • FintechCircle: paid enrolments for the April and May 2026 cohorts, or its accounts for the year to 31 Jul 2026 (due by 30 Apr 2027).
  • Either client could simply state its monthly revenue band; that beats every estimate here.
